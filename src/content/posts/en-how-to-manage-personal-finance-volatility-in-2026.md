## Understanding the Spending Roller Coaster  
Volatility stems from the mix of fixed expenses (rent, phone plan) and variable ones (food, entertainment, unexpected events). Fixed [costs](/en/posts/en-private-pension-2026-is-it-worth-it-practical-guide) are predictable; variable costs can change from one day to the next. When variable spending spikes suddenly, the month’s balance can flip from “surplus” to “shortfall.” These swings often line up with seasonal changes, vacations, or aggressive promotions that trigger impulse buying.

## How Volatility Affects Your Budget  
- **Erodes your emergency fund:** Unexpected costs eat into what should be your safety net.  
- **Bill delays:** When the balance fluctuates, you’re more likely to miss a payment, incurring fees and [interest](/en/glossario/en-interest).  
- **Financial stress:** Constantly feeling “in the red” drains mental energy and can affect other areas of life.  

Recognizing these impacts is the first step toward building a plan that absorbs the variations without derailing your long‑term goals.
