Imagine this: you get home after a hectic day, open the drawer where you keep receipts, and find a pile of grocery slips, fuel receipts, and [credit](/en/glossario/en-credit)‑card statements. Each piece looks like a tiny mystery, and when you try to add them up, it feels like the money vanishes before the month ends. That chaos fuels sleepless nights and the feeling that you’ll never have enough for the next vacation or an [emergency fund](/en/glossario/en-emergency-fund).

That’s exactly where FinMoovi steps in. The app has **smart capture**: just snap a photo of the latest receipt with your phone, and in seconds it reads the amounts, categorizes the expense, and drops it into your tracking table. In under five minutes you’ll have a clear picture of where every cent is going. Try it now: open FinMoovi, tap **Add expense**, choose **Capture by photo**, snap the receipt, and let the app do the rest.

## Turn expenses into visual data  

A table doesn’t have to be a maze of numbers. Think of it as a visual compass. Start with four basic columns: **Category**, **Monthly Goal**, **Actual Spend**, and **Difference**. Each row represents a type of expense – food, transport, entertainment, fixed bills. When the app automatically fills the **Actual Spend** column, you only need to set a realistic goal, such as “spend no more than the cost of two meals out per week.” That instant view instantly shows where the budget is slipping.

## Build your own savings table  

1. Open the app and go to the **Monthly [Planning](/en/posts/en-budgeting-with-minimum-wage-practical-tips)** section.  
2. Create a new sheet and give it a name that makes sense, like **[Savings](/en/glossario/en-savings) 2026**.  
3. Add the categories that eat up most of your money.  
4. Set realistic goals based on your lifestyle – for example, “don’t exceed the cost of one coffee per day.”  
5. Save and let FinMoovi automatically update the numbers as you log them.

![Build your own savings table](/images/posts/como-montar-sua-tabela-de-economia-2026-e-controlar-gastos-2.webp)

Keep the table lean: the fewer columns, the easier the daily review. When the **Difference** turns negative, the app sends a subtle alert so you can rethink that purchase.

**Practical tip:** **Block a 10‑minute slot every Monday to review the table; tweak goals and flag items you can cut, like subscriptions you barely use.** This quick ritual stops tiny excesses from piling up over the month.

## Use monthly goals to stay focused  

Goals are the engine of change. In FinMoovi you can create targets like “save the equivalent of one month’s rent” or “cut streaming costs to half the price of a cinema ticket.” Once a goal is set, the app generates a progress chart that sits on the home screen. Every time you log an expense, the chart updates, instantly showing whether you’re on track or need to trim a bit more.

## Stop impulse buys with alerts  

One of the biggest personal‑finance villains is the impulse purchase. FinMoovi lets you turn on alerts that fire when you try to log an expense outside the range you set for a category. For instance, if your entertainment goal equals the cost of one dinner out per week and you attempt to log a concert ticket that costs twice that amount, the app warns: “This expense exceeds your entertainment goal.” That gentle nudge is often enough to make you pause and reconsider.

![Avoid impulse purchases with alerts](/images/posts/como-montar-sua-tabela-de-economia-2026-e-controlar-gastos-4.webp)

## Adjust your plan as results come in  

The table isn’t static. At the end of each week, glance at the cash‑flow [reports](/en/posts/en-do-you-also-lose-track-of-where-your-money-went-at-the-end) FinMoovi generates automatically. They show the share of each category in total spending, letting you quickly spot which areas are overrunning the plan. If food is costing you the equivalent of three coffees a day, it might be time to revise your grocery list or try a simpler menu.

Additionally, FinMoovi’s **[multi‑currency](/en/posts/en-do-you-lose-track-of-your-spending-when-traveling-using)** feature ensures that if you receive income or make purchases in another currency, everything is converted and displayed in the same table, keeping your control consistent regardless of where the money comes from.
