## See Where the Money Is Slipping

Most people underestimate the impact of recurring small purchases. A daily coffee, a quick snack, or a subscription you barely use can add up over a year to the equivalent of a minimum‑wage salary. When those amounts join fixed costs like rent, transport, and internet, the budget tightens without you noticing the source of the imbalance.

The key to changing this is turning [cash flow](/en/glossario/en-cash-flow) into something visual. When you see, on a simple chart, how much each category consumes, it’s easier to spot patterns and decide where to cut. This “outside‑in” view shatters the illusion that everything is under control while money silently disappears between the lines.

## Three Steps to Stop the Waste

1. **Capture everything** – Use a receipt photo or voice note to log each purchase instantly. Don’t rely on memory.  
2. **Set monthly limits** – Create targets for each category (food, entertainment, transport). FinMoovi lets you adjust these limits anytime.  
3. **Review twice a week** – Spend 15 minutes, two times a week, looking at the report. Identify the category closest to its limit and think of a cheaper alternative or eliminate the expense.

**Practical tip:** When you get a “near limit” notification, open the shopping‑list module; it shows the real‑time total and suggests items you can drop to keep the budget on track.
