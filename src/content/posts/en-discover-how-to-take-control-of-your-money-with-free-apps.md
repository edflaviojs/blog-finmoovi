Imagine the scene: you get home, the kitchen table is covered with papers – grocery receipts, phone bills, [credit](/en/glossario/en-credit)‑card statements. Each one represents a cent that vanished without explanation, and the feeling that money is slipping through your fingers grows stronger every day. This mess creates anxiety and makes it impossible to see where your salary is going, let alone plan for the future or close the month without tight spots.

It’s exactly in this chaos that most people realize they need a simple [financial control](/en/posts/en-do-you-also-lose-track-of-where-your-money-went-at-the-end) tool. Instead of building complicated spreadsheets or writing everything down by hand, just use your phone’s camera to record each expense and let the technology do the rest. FinMoovi, a free [finance](/en/posts/en-do-you-lose-track-of-your-spending-when-traveling-using) app, offers this solution naturally: snap a photo of a receipt or use a voice command, and it categorizes the expense automatically, turning the pile of papers into clear, actionable data. To get started, set aside five minutes, open the app, take a picture of the latest receipt, and watch the category appear instantly.

## Set savings goals

With the app, creating goals is as easy as writing a reminder. Follow these steps:

![Create savings goals](/images/posts/descubra-como-controlar-seu-dinheiro-com-apps-gratuitos-3.webp)

1. Go to the “Goals” section and pick an objective – for example, “[Emergency fund](/en/glossario/en-emergency-fund) equal to three months of salary.”  
2. Set a timeframe (e.g., 12 months) and the total amount you want to reach.  
3. The app calculates how much you need to save each period and adds that amount as a “future expense” in your cash‑flow view.  
4. Receive weekly alerts when you’re close to hitting the goal or if you risk falling off track.

**Practical tip:** start with small goals, like “save the equivalent of one coffee per day.” Success with modest targets builds confidence for bigger challenges.

## Spend just 5 minutes a day

Dedicate only five minutes each day to keep your finances on track:

1. **Open the app** right after dinner or before bed.  
2. **Capture** the latest receipts or use voice to log the day’s expenses.  
3. **Check** the cash‑flow summary – see if any expense fell outside the expected category.  
4. **Adjust** goals or reminders if needed.  
5. **Close** with a quick glance at the progress charts; this reinforces a sense of control over your money.

Turning this tiny ritual into a habit creates a layer of peace of mind that stops small purchases from turning into big month‑end surprises.
