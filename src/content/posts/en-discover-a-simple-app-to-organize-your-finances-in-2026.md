## Set monthly goals and curb impulse buys

Impulse purchases are a major cause of financial chaos. FinMoovi includes a **Monthly [Planning](/en/posts/en-budgeting-with-minimum-wage-practical-tips)** module that lets you set spending limits per category. Say you decide not to spend more than the price of a coffee a day on snacks; just set that limit. When the total in that category reaches 80 % of the cap, the app sends a friendly reminder to rein in the spending.

The **Shopping Mode** feature also lets you build a grocery list before you head to the store. As you add items, the app shows the running total in real time, helping you stay within budget.

**Practical tip:** Before your next big shopping trip, open Shopping Mode, add everything you plan to buy, and see if the total fits within the goal you set. Trim the list if needed.
