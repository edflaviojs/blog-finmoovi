## Why expense control often slips

Most people try to manage spending with spreadsheets or handwritten notes, but those methods demand constant discipline and quickly become a burden. When tracking relies on memory or daily effort, it’s easy to let tiny purchases slip by—purchases that, added up, can equal the cost of a rent payment or a daily coffee. Moreover, the lack of a quick visual overview prevents you from spotting consumption patterns and adjusting habits before they turn into a bigger problem.

## Simple strategies to organize expenses

1. **Group by visual categories** – Use different colors for each type of spending (e.g., green for food, blue for transport). FinMoovi lets you customize these colors, making the dashboard easy to read.  
2. **Set monthly limits** – Define a ceiling for each category. When you’re close to the limit, the app sends a discreet alert, helping you avoid overspending.  
3. **Review weekly** – Reserve 10 minutes at the end of each week to glance at the summary. This short review prevents surprises at month‑end.
