In the middle of that whirlwind, the latest receipt ends up forgotten in a drawer, and when the due date pops up, panic sets in. This lack of organization turns the daily life of anyone who works for themselves into a constant guessing game.

That situation is more common than you think, but the solution could be just a few clicks away. Spend five minutes right now, open the app, snap a photo of the last receipt on your desk, and let FinMoovi classify the expense.**

## Map your income and expenses
1. **List every source of income**: completed projects, recurring gigs, royalties.  
2. **Record each inflow** in FinMoovi as soon as the money hits your account. Use the [multi‑currency](/en/posts/en-digital-bank-vs-major-bank-which-is-worth-it-in-2026) feature if you receive payments in different currencies.  
3. **Capture every outflow**: when you buy supplies, pay a service, or receive an invoice, take a photo or dictate the amount. The app handles the categorisation automatically, [saving](/en/posts/en-how-to-build-a-budget-that-actually-works-in-2026) you time.

### Practical tip  
**Whenever you receive or pay something, log it immediately; this prevents information pile‑up and keeps your cash‑flow picture accurate.**

In FinMoovi, use the **Monthly [Planning](/en/posts/en-budgeting-with-minimum-wage-practical-tips) / Goals** feature to create targets like “save the equivalent of one month’s rent” or “build a three‑month emergency fund.” Each goal triggers an alert when you’re close to reaching it, preserving peace of mind.

## Use smart capture to log expenses
Smart capture removes the need to write everything down by hand. Just point your phone’s camera at a receipt or say “I paid for three coffees,” and the app recognises the amount, date and category. It works offline and syncs when you’re back online, ensuring no expense slips through.

## Review and adjust weekly
Set aside a moment at the end of each week to look at the cash‑flow report that FinMoovi generates automatically. Spot categories that are over budget and tweak upcoming spending. This short review prevents surprises and keeps control in your hands.

## Start with 5 minutes a day
1. **Open FinMoovi** and check the balance of your main account.  
2. **Capture** any expenses that haven’t been logged yet (income, receipt, quick purchase).  
3. **Check the goals**: see if any are near completion and mark them as achieved.

5. **Close** with a positive note: celebrate today’s organisation.
