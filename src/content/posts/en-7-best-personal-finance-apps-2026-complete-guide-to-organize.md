## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does photo receipt capture work?  
Just snap a picture of the receipt; the algorithm extracts the amount, date, and category, inserting the expense automatically.

### Is it safe to store my data in the cloud?  
Yes, provided the app uses end‑to‑end encryption and offers two‑factor authentication, meeting international privacy standards.

### Can I use the app without an internet connection?  
Apps like FinMoovi and SpendWise have full offline modes, letting you log expenses that sync once you’re back online.

### What’s the difference between free and premium plans?  
Free versions usually limit the number of linked accounts, show ads, or offer basic features only. Premium unlocks advanced reports, removes ads, and gives priority support.
