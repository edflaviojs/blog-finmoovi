## The hidden cost of a manual spreadsheet

Most of us have been there: the month ends, you open the spreadsheet you’ve been using for years and notice dozens of missing rows, duplicate categories, and some expenses that never made it in. You scramble to recall every purchase from the past week – that quick snack, an unexpected cleaning‑supply run, a subscription that renewed without warning – and end up spending more time fixing the spreadsheet than actually saving money.

That “open, type, fix” loop creates three main problems:

1. **Time loss** – Each entry takes about two to three minutes. Multiply that by 20 weekly purchases and you’re looking at almost an hour just to update numbers.  
2. **Typos** – One extra or missing “0” can skew your balance, leading you to cut a cost that’s actually within budget.  
3. **Demotivation** – When a tool meant to simplify life becomes a chore, you’re likely to abandon tracking and be surprised by month‑end bills.

According to the *World Bank*, adopting digital financial‑management tools can cut administrative time by up to 30 % (World Bank, 2022). Yet many people remain stuck with the old spreadsheet habit.

## Tips to get the most out of voice capture

- **Speak clearly** and keep it simple (“I spent $6 on transport”).
