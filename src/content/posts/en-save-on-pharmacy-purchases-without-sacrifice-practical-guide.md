## Prioritize the Essentials, Cut the Extras  

Most shoppers buy more than they truly need when they reach the pharmacy. Pull up your purchase list from the past three months (FinMoovi generates this history with one click) and spot items that appear often but aren’t indispensable. Ask yourself:

- **Do I really need this product?**  
- **Is there a cheaper or generic alternative?**  
- **Can I buy in bulk and lower the unit cost?**  

Answering these questions trims the impulse to grab “maybe useful” items and keeps the focus on what actually [benefits](/en/posts/en-cdb-vs-treasury-selic-which-yields-more-in-2026) your health.
