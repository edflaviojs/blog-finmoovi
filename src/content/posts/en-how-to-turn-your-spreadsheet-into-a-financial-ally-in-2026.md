## Basic spreadsheet layout  

Start by creating a sheet called **“Monthly Summary.”** In it, set up columns for:

- **Category** (housing, food, transport, entertainment, etc.)  
- **Planned amount** – what you expect to spend in that category, using references like “about one month’s rent” or “the cost of a daily coffee.”  
- **Actual amount** – the total you actually spent, which you’ll fill in as the month progresses.  

At the bottom, add a simple formula that calculates the difference between planned and actual. This quick view instantly shows where you’re on track and where you might need to cut back.

## Automating alerts and goals  

Financial discipline often hinges on tiny reminders. In Excel, use conditional formatting: if the actual amount exceeds 110 % of the planned amount, the cell turns red. That instant signal acts like a “watch out, overspending” alert.
