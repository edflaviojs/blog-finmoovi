## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does a 0.14 % rise in the IPCA mean?  
It’s a monthly increase in the overall price index, indicating that, on average, the goods and services consumed by Brazilians were 0.14 % more expensive in October.  

### Why did food prices rise more than everything else?  
The 1.04 % increase reflects pressure on food and beverage prices, such as meat, fruit, and ready‑made meals, which are essential items and sensitive to harvest variations and production costs.  

### How does the housing decline affect my budget?  
The negative -0.51 % variation temporarily lowers rents, electricity bills, and condo fees, easing monthly expenses, but it does not fully offset the overall price increase.
