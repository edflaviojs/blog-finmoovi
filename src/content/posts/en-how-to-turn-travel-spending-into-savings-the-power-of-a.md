## Avoid common pitfalls when converting currencies  

* **Hidden fees** – Some sites show the “interbank” rate and then add a [spread](/en/glossario/en-spread). Choose converters that display the final rate you’ll actually pay on your card.  
* **Stale updates** – Exchange rates can shift in minutes. Use tools that refresh every five minutes or less; FinMoovi syncs automatically each time you open the app.  
* **Excessive rounding** – After conversion, double‑check that the amount hasn’t been rounded up. The app lets you edit the number before saving.
