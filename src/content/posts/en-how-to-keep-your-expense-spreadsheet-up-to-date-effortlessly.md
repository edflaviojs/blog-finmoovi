These points create a vicious cycle: the more the sheet lags, the less trust you have in it, and the less motivation you have to use it.

### How FinMoovi’s AI categorizes your expenses
The tech behind Smart Capture uses machine‑learning models trained on millions of receipts and transaction descriptions. When you send a photo, the algorithm extracts:
- **Amount** (detects numbers and currency symbols)  
- **Date** (recognizes date patterns or uses the device timestamp)  
- **Description** (text on the receipt or recognized voice)

Based on these data points, the app suggests the most likely category. If you usually eat out on Tuesdays, the AI learns that and automatically tags the entry as “Food – Restaurants.” If the suggestion is off, just drag the expense to the correct category – the model keeps learning.
