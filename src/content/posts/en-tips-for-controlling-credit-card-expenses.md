## Expense Control
Imagine you earn $1,000 per month and spend $300 on fixed expenses, such as rent, electricity, and internet. This means you have $700 available to spend on other things, like food, leisure, and shopping. However, if you don't control your expenses, it's easy to exceed this limit and end up with [credit card](/en/posts/en-credit-card-vs-debit-card-which-is-worth-it-in-2026) debt.

One way to avoid this is to create a [budget](/en/posts/en-5-tips-to-reorganize-your-finances-on-vacation) and track your expenses. **Practical tip:** You can use a [financial management](/en/posts/en-5-alternatives-to-organizze-in-2026-options-to-manage-your-f) app to record all your expenses and have a clear view of where your [money](/en/posts/en-avoid-impulse-purchases) is going. For example, if you spend $100 per month on dining out, you can try to reduce that amount to $60 and save $40.

## Credit Card Expenses
Credit cards can be a useful tool for making online or in-store purchases, but they can also be a trap for those who don't know how to control their expenses. It's essential to remember that a credit card is not free [money](/en/posts/en-creating-an-emergency-fund) and that you'll need to pay all expenses made with [interest](/en/glossario/en-interest) and fees.

To avoid problems, it's crucial to have a spending limit on your credit card and not exceed it. Additionally, it's essential to pay your credit card bill on time and not let interest accumulate. You can visit the [central bank](https://www.bcb.gov.br) website to learn more about dealing with credit cards and debt.

## [Savings](/en/posts/en-how-to-avoid-impulse-purchases-and-protect-your-wallet) Tips
There are many ways to save money and reduce credit card expenses. Here are some tips:
* Shop at discount stores or during sales
* Cook at home instead of eating out
* Use public transportation or a bike instead of a car
* Cancel subscriptions to services you don't use

**Practical tip:** You can create an [emergency fund](/en/glossario/en-emergency-fund) to cover unexpected expenses and avoid having to use your credit card. For example, if you save $200 per month, you'll have $2,400 in a year, which can be enough to cover unexpected expenses.
