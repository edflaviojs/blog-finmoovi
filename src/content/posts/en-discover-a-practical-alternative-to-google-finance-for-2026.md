## Why Google Finance isn’t enough anymore

Google Finance is still handy for checking stock quotes and market news, but it falls short in three critical areas for [personal finance](/en/posts/en-30-day-rule-for-purchases):

![Why Google Finance isn’t enough](/images/posts/descubra-uma-alternativa-ao-google-finance-para-2026-2.webp)

1. **Limited integration** – no direct connection to bank accounts or credit cards, forcing you to enter data manually.  
2. **No expense tracking** – the tool focuses only on investment assets, ignoring daily [cash flow](/en/glossario/en-cash-flow).  
3. **Fragmented user experience** – hopping between charts, news, and lists can be confusing, especially on mobile devices.

These gaps create a “forget‑the‑small‑spends” effect, where unnoticed little purchases add up to the cost of an entire month’s rent.

## How FinMoovi solves those problems

### Smart capture + automatic categorization  

Snap a receipt and the algorithm reads the text, identifies the merchant, and classifies the spend (food, transport, entertainment, etc.). Prefer to speak? Just say “$30 on gasoline” and the app logs it instantly. This automation eliminates bulky spreadsheets and cuts human error.

### Consolidated view of investments and expenses  

The main dashboard shows two side‑by‑side columns: one summarizing investments ([stocks](/en/glossario/en-stocks), CDs, funds) and the other summarizing monthly expenses. Bar and pie charts are interactive—tap “food” and the app displays the trend over the past three months, helping you spot consumption patterns.

### Personalized goals and alerts  

Set a goal like “save the equivalent of a daily coffee for the next three months.” FinMoovi sends a discreet alert when an unexpected expense threatens the goal, prompting you to rethink the purchase before confirming.

**Practical tip:** use Shopping mode to build your grocery list. As you add items, the app totals the cost in real time. If the amount exceeds the “monthly [spending limit](/en/glossario/en-spending-limit)” you defined, it warns you and suggests cheaper [alternatives](/en/posts/en-5-alternatives-to-organizze-in-2026-options-to-manage-your).
