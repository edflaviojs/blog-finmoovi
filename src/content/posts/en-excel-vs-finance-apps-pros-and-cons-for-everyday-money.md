## 1. Why spreadsheets are still so popular?

### Flexibility and personalization  
- **Full customization**: You build any structure you want – columns, charts, complex formulas.  
- **Zero cost**: Most spreadsheet tools (Google Sheets, LibreOffice) are free.  
- **Absolute control**: Every cell belongs to you, with no mandatory fields or limited categories.

### When that helps  
- **One‑off projects**: Trip planning, event budgeting, or tracking a specific [investment](/en/glossario/en-investment).  
- **Advanced users**: If you already master formulas, pivot tables and macros, you can extract insights that a standard app can’t provide.

### Practical limitations  
- **Manual upkeep**: Entering every [expense](/en/glossario/en-expense), updating categories and building new reports takes time.  
- **Restricted access**: Usually works best on a computer; moving the file to a phone risks loss or outdated versions.

## 2. When a finance app makes more sense

### Automation and mobility  
- **Smart capture**: Snap a receipt photo or say “I spent $15 on lunch” and the app logs it instantly. FinMoovi, for example, uses AI to categorize expenses in seconds.  
- **Multi‑currency**: If you earn in dollars and spend in euros, the app converts everything in real time, no formulas needed.  
- **Alerts and reminders**: Bill due notices, recurring payment alerts and [savings](/en/glossario/en-savings)‑goal reminders arrive straight to your phone.

## 3. Quick checklist: Spreadsheet or App?

| **Spreadsheet is right for you if…** | **App is right for you if…** |
|--------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| You enjoy building custom formulas | You prefer logging expenses in seconds, without typing |
| Your tracking is occasional (e.g., a 3‑month project) | You need daily monitoring, even offline |
| You don’t want a subscription and prefer free/open‑source tools | You value bill reminders and balance alerts |
| You’re comfortable with Excel/Sheets and like raw data views | You want ready‑made charts, automatic goals and instant reports |
| You use only one computer for everything | You want access on phone, tablet or laptop simultaneously |
| Manual updates don’t bother you | You want the app to capture receipts by photo or voice |

If most answers fall on the “app” side, it may be time to give one a try.
