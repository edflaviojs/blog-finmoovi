## Understanding the Power of Small Actions  
Tiny habit tweaks have a bigger impact than we often realize. When you log every expense automatically, you create a visual cash‑flow map. That map lets you spot patterns, cut excesses, and, most importantly, set realistic goals. The aim isn’t to overhaul your life overnight, but to introduce simple steps that add up to financial peace of mind.

Another simple yet powerful action is to define monthly goals.
