## The chaos of receipts in your pocket  

You’ve just left the grocery store, the cart still smells of fresh bread, and your bag is full of paper: a receipt, a discount coupon, a bakery slip. When you open your [finance](/en/glossario/en-finance) app, what usually happens? You pull up a spreadsheet, try to remember where each [expense](/en/glossario/en-expense) belongs, and end up spending more time organizing than actually [saving](/en/posts/en-how-to-build-a-budget-that-actually-works-in-2026). This daily confusion is what most people feel when they try to keep a realistic budget.  

The simple answer? Let artificial intelligence do the heavy lifting. Automatic expense categorization with AI recognizes each purchase, assigns it to a category (food, transport, entertainment…) and updates your dashboard in real time. In a few clicks you get a clear view of where your money is going—no typing required.  

## How the AI understands each expense  

### 1. Smart capture  
Using your phone’s camera or a voice command, you snap a photo of the receipt or say “coffee expense.” The computer‑vision algorithm reads the text, identifies the merchant and the amount, and turns everything into structured data.  

![How the AI understands each expense](/images/posts/categorizacao-automatica-de-gastos-com-ia-como-deixar-suas-2.webp)  

### 2. Pattern learning  
The AI compares the recognized text with a database of thousands of already‑categorized merchants. If “Starbucks” appears, it already knows the likely category is “Coffee & Drinks.” When it encounters something new, it asks you in a short notification: “Was this expense food or entertainment?” Your answer is stored to improve future accuracy.  

### 3. Multi‑currency without hassle  
If the receipt is in dollars, euros or another currency, the automatic conversion engine applies the day’s rate (source: World Bank). So even travelers or international shoppers see everything in a single view.
