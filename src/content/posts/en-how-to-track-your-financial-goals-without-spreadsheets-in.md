## The pain of not seeing progress  

Most of us have dreams: a year‑end beach vacation, a car that makes daily commuting easier, or an emergency fund that gives peace of mind when the unexpected hits. In practice, those goals get stuck in piles of receipts, random phone notes, and spreadsheets nobody updates. Some red flags that things are out of control:  

- **Spending vs. goal disconnect** – You pay rent, buy lunch, but have no idea how much is left for the trip.  
- **Last‑minute adjustments** – When you realize the goal is far off, you have to cut everything suddenly, which creates stress.  
- **No clear view** – Confusing charts or tables full of numbers that don’t tell you anything quickly.  

According to the *World Bank*, 56 % of households that don’t monitor their spending are more likely to face unexpected financial emergencies. In other words, lack of tracking isn’t just annoying; it can be risky.
