## What are recurring expenses?

Recurring expenses are payments that happen on a regular schedule – monthly, bimonthly, yearly – and are usually set up as automatic debit or auto‑renewal. They include:

- Streaming, music, or digital magazine subscriptions  
- Mobile, internet, and cable TV plans  
- Gym, club, or coworking memberships  
- Subscription boxes for food, beauty products, or books  
- Insurance, [loan](/en/glossario/en-loan) [installments](/en/glossario/en-installments), and [financing](/en/glossario/en-financing) payments  

These costs are predictable, but they can become a problem when they pile up or when you lose track of how much you’re spending on them.

## Why do they mess up your budget?

1. **The “invisible” effect** – Because they don’t show up in daily spending, it’s easy to underestimate their total impact.  
2. **Automatic renewals** – Canceling often requires a few clicks or a phone call, so many people keep paying for services they no longer use.  
3. **Misalignment with income** – If recurring expenses take up a large chunk of your earnings, little is left for emergencies or investments.

Recurring expenses repeat but aren’t always essential (e.g., a streaming subscription). Fixed expenses are mandatory costs like rent or a loan payment.
