### Understand What a Mortgage Really Is  
A mortgage isn’t just “a big loan.” It has three main components: the principal payment (fixed or adjustable), homeowner’s insurance, and the interest that accrues on the remaining balance. When you look only at the total debt amount, you lose sight of how each piece affects your monthly budget. By separating these items in FinMoovi, you can see exactly how much of your income is tied up and even model the impact of an extra principal payment.

### Treat the Payment Like a Monthly Goal  
Most people treat the mortgage payment as an unavoidable expense. Turn it into a **monthly goal** inside the app: create a “Home Mortgage” category and set a limit that matches the payment amount. FinMoovi will send alerts when your account balance gets close to dropping below what’s needed to cover the payment, preventing nasty surprises on due dates. This practice brings peace of mind and lets you tweak other spending (like streaming services or dining out) before the bill arrives.

**Practical tip:** As soon as you receive the first statement, record it with the photo capture. Then go to “Goals” and set a “pay mortgage” target. When the app signals you’re on track, celebrate with a small treat that doesn’t hurt the budget—maybe an extra coffee.

### Keep Track of the Extra Costs That Come With a Mortgage  
Insurance, notary fees, and condo maintenance are expenses that pop up along the way. They’re often due on different dates than the mortgage payment, creating “invisible costs.” In FinMoovi, create sub‑categories under “Home Mortgage”: “Insurance,” “Fees,” and “Condo.” Use the reminder feature to get notifications a few days before each due date. That way you’ll never be caught off guard again.

### Use the Multi‑Currency View if You Earn in Another Currency  
If part of your income comes from remote work or investments in a different currency, FinMoovi lets you monitor everything on one screen, automatically converting to the currency you use daily. This helps you see, for example, how much of your dollar salary actually covers the local‑currency mortgage payment, preventing unexpected shortfalls.

### Build an Extra‑Principal Repayment Plan Without the Headache  
Paying down the loan early reduces the total interest you’ll pay, but many don’t know where to start. In FinMoovi, simply create an “Extra Repayment Goal” with the amount you want to apply each quarter. The app will show in real time how that reduces the [outstanding balance](/en/glossario/en-outstanding-balance) and shortens the loan term. Seeing the impact often motivates you to divert even the equivalent of a daily coffee toward extra principal.

## Frequently Asked Questions  

### What happens if I miss a payment?  
A missed payment adds late‑payment interest and can hurt your [credit score](/en/glossario/en-credit-score), as well as increase the outstanding balance. FinMoovi warns you in advance so you can reshuffle other expenses and avoid the miss.

### How can I tell if my mortgage is cheap or expensive?  
Compare the annual percentage rate (APR) of your contract with the average long‑term loan rates published by reputable sources such as [Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com). If your rate is well above the average, it may be time to renegotiate.
