## Smart lighting: swap old bulbs for LEDs  

Most homes still use incandescent or fluorescent bulbs that waste energy. Replacing each light fixture with a good‑quality LED can cut consumption by up to 80 % while keeping the same brightness.

**Practical tip:** **Install timers or motion sensors in high‑traffic areas** (hallways, bathrooms). The light will only turn on when there’s movement, avoiding minutes of wasted illumination.

In FinMoovi, log the replacement date and set a monthly goal to “Reduce lighting consumption.” The app will send alerts when the “Energy” category drops, reinforcing the habit.

## Appliances that use less power  

Refrigerators, air conditioners, and washing machines are the biggest culprits on the electricity bill. Choosing models with an energy‑efficiency label (A rating or higher) can mean the difference between paying for an extra daily coffee or keeping your budget balanced.

![Appliances that use less power](/images/posts/como-cortar-a-conta-de-luz-e-ganhar-tranquilidade-2.webp)

- **Refrigerator:** keep the temperature between 3 °C and 5 °C; avoid leaving the door open for long periods.  
- **Air conditioner:** use the “eco” mode and set the temperature around 23 °C; clean the filters regularly.  
- **Washing machine:** opt for short cycles and cold water, which consume less electricity.

With the smart‑capture feature, photograph the appliance’s efficiency label and FinMoovi will create a record that makes it easy to compare consumption before and after the upgrade.

## Harness natural light  

Sunlight is free, and when used wisely it dramatically reduces the need for artificial lighting. Position work desks near windows, open curtains at sunrise, and use solar screens to prevent overheating on hot days.

![Harness natural light](/images/posts/como-cortar-a-conta-de-luz-e-ganhar-tranquilidade-3.webp)

Additionally, installing a small solar panel system—even just to power exterior lighting—can turn part of your electricity bill into income. While the upfront cost is comparable to a modest car, the payback usually appears within a few years, easing the monthly bill’s weight.
