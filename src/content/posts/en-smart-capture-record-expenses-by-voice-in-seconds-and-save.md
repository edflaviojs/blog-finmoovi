## The problem nobody has time to admit  

You’ve just left the grocery store, the cart is still full of bags, and the last thing on your mind is pulling out your phone to jot down every item. Meanwhile, the checkout beeps, the delivery driver knocks, and in your head the day’s agenda is already running: meeting, taking the kid to swim class, replying to that urgent email. In the middle of that rush, the receipt ends up on the kitchen counter and, as usual, the expense entry gets postponed—or simply forgotten. The result? An incomplete spreadsheet, messy categories, and at month‑end the feeling that money “disappears” without explanation.  

## How voice capture solves everything with one tap  

Smart voice capture works like a personal assistant that understands what you just bought and instantly puts the information into your [finance](/en/glossario/en-finance) manager. Say “I spent $15 on whole‑grain bread,” and the app recognizes the number, the currency and the description, automatically classifies the expense (e.g., “Food”), and saves it to your history. The technology blends speech‑to‑text with machine‑learning categorization algorithms. No typing, no photo of the receipt, no opening a spreadsheet; just speak.  

![How voice capture solves everything with one tap](/images/posts/smart-capture-registre-gastos-por-voz-em-segundos-e-ganhe-2.webp)

This approach brings three clear [benefits](/en/posts/en-cdb-vs-treasury-selic-which-yields-more-in-2026):  

1. **Speed** – Recording takes less than 3 seconds even in noisy environments, thanks to noise‑filtering and finance‑specific language models.  
2. **Accuracy** – The algorithm matches the keyword “bread” with the “Food” category and, if unsure, suggests the best option before confirming.  
3. **Consistency** – Every expense is instantly tied to a date, time and category, eliminating the gaps that usually appear when you log later.

## Tips to get the most out of voice capture  

1. **Speak clearly and briefly** – “I spent $20 on a book” works better than [long](/en/glossario/en-long) sentences.  
2. **Use consistent keywords** – always mention the currency and the type of expense; this trains the algorithm to learn your pattern.  
3. **Review the first few entries** – when you log a new kind of expense, check the suggested category and adjust if needed. After that, the app “remembers.”  
4. **[Leverage](/en/glossario/en-leverage) reminders** – enable balance alerts to be notified when spending exceeds a category’s limit.  
5. **Combine with photos** – if you want to keep the receipt, snap a picture and use voice capture at the same time; the app links the image to the entry.

## Frequently asked questions  

### How does voice recognition handle different languages?  
The algorithm supports the major languages (English, Spanish, Portuguese) and detects the mentioned currency, adjusting the classification automatically.  

### What if I speak the wrong amount?  
The app shows the transcription before saving. Just correct the number or category and confirm; the change is saved in real time.  

### Does voice capture work offline?  
Yes. Basic recognition runs without a connection, and data syncs to the cloud once you’re back online.  

### Can I use voice capture for recurring expenses?  
Absolutely. Record the first expense by voice and enable the “repeat monthly” option. The app will automatically create subsequent entries.
