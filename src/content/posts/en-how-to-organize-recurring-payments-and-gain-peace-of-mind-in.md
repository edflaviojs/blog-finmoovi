## Plan Your Monthly Cash Flow  

A well‑structured [cash flow](/en/glossario/en-cash-flow) includes not only [fixed income](/en/glossario/en-fixed-income) and expenses but also recurring payments that, while predictable, can still be tweaked. In FinMoovi, the **cash flow** section shows a simple bar chart where each bar represents money coming in or going out on a given day. Automatic charges appear in distinct colors, making it easy to spot spending spikes.

By reviewing this view, you can anticipate periods of higher financial pressure – for example, when the internet bill and gym plan fall on the same week – and shift less essential expenses to keep the balance healthy.
