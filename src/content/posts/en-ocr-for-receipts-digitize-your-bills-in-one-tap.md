## How OCR technology works in plain language  

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) is the tech that “reads” text from images. When you snap a photo of a receipt, the algorithm identifies characters, lines and tables, converts everything into editable text, and then matches each item to an [expense category](/en/glossario/en-expense-category) (food, transport, health, etc.). The process happens in three steps:

![How OCR technology works](/images/posts/ocr-para-notas-fiscais-digitalize-recibos-em-1-toque-2.webp)

1. **Image capture** – The app uses the camera or your photo library to receive the picture. Pre‑processing algorithms adjust brightness and contrast, ensuring the text is readable even on crumpled receipts.  
2. **Text recognition** – Machine‑learning models analyze the image, extract numbers and words, and create a “skeleton” of data.  
3. **Automatic classification** – Based on rules and an AI engine, the system assigns each line to a predefined category such as “food” or “bills”.

All of this happens in a few seconds, with no typing required. The technology is well documented in sources like the [Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/o/ocr.asp) article and is used across industries from banking to healthcare.

## Tips to get the most out of OCR  

1. **Good lighting** – Shoot in well‑lit areas; natural light reduces shadows and improves readability.  
2. **Focus on the total** – Make sure the receipt’s total amount is visible; the algorithm often uses this field as a reference.  
3. **Quick review** – After capture, glance at the suggested fields; adjusting a category takes less than 5 seconds.  
4. **Use offline mode** – If you’re traveling without internet, FinMoovi stores the photo and processes OCR once you’re back online, ensuring no receipt stays “pending”.
