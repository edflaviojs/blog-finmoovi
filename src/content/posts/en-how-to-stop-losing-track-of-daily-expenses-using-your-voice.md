## Why Hand‑Writing Every Expense No Longer Works

- **Constant distraction** – While you’re on a call or answering emails, the chance to remember to log a purchase disappears.  
- **Scarce time** – Typing each amount takes about 15 seconds. Multiply that by five purchases and you’ve lost almost two minutes – time you could be spending with family.  
- **Transcription errors** – Writing numbers by hand makes it easy to swap a “5” for a “6,” distorting your budget.  
- **Lack of motivation** – The feeling of “one more task” leads to procrastination, piling up expenses and delivering a surprise at month‑end.  

According to the *World Bank*, more than 30 % of households in advanced economies report difficulty tracking everyday spending, which directly hampers their ability to save. The solution has to be fast, practical, and almost automatic.

## Tips to Get Even More Out of Voice Capture

1. **Keep phrases short and direct** – “Coffee, $2.5” works better than “I paid for a coffee that cost about two dollars and fifty cents.”  
2. **Speak in a quiet setting** – traffic noise can interfere with recognition.  
3. **Leverage automatic categorization** – mentioning keywords like “supermarket,” “electric bill,” automatically routes the entry to the right group.  
4. **Review weekly** – open the weekly report to tweak goals and spot spending patterns.
