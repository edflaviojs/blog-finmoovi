## 3. Quick checklist: spreadsheet or app?  

### A spreadsheet is right for you if…  

- You enjoy building **custom charts** and digging into detailed trends.  
- Your budget is **extremely specific** (e.g., tax calculations by category).  
- You have **technical know‑how** to write macros or use scripts.  
- You prefer to **keep data locally** and don’t want to rely on an internet connection.  
- You don’t mind entering expenses **manually** every day.  

### An app is right for you if…  

- You want to **record expenses the instant they occur**, using photo or voice.  
- You need **quick access** on phone, tablet or computer without opening spreadsheets.  
- You value **alerts and reminders** that stop missed payments or blown‑out bills.  
- You like to **see monthly goals** and track progress with ready‑made graphs.  
- You deal with **different currencies** or have recurring expenses that need monitoring.  

If most of the items under “App” describe your situation, it’s time to consider the switch.
