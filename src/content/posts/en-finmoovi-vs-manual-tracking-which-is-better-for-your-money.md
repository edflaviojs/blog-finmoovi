## 1. Why do we still use paper and spreadsheets?

Most people started tracking money before smartphones existed. Hand‑writing or using a spreadsheet has a few apparent perks:

* **Low cost** – no subscription and no internet needed.  
* **Absolute privacy** – nothing leaves your notebook or hard drive.  
* **Full flexibility** – you create categories, subtotals and formulas exactly how you want.

But these [benefits](/en/posts/en-cdb-vs-treasury-selic-which-yields-more-in-2026) come with drawbacks that become painful over time:

| Drawback | How it affects daily life |
|----------|---------------------------|
| **Recording time** | Every purchase requires writing or typing, stealing minutes that could be used elsewhere. |
| **Human error** | Typing “300” instead of “30” or forgetting an expense makes reports inaccurate. |
| **Fragmented view** | Data scattered across multiple tabs or sheets makes a global analysis hard and slows decision‑making. |
| **Data security** | A corrupted file or a lost notebook can mean losing your entire financial history. |

These points drive many people toward digital solutions like FinMoovi.

## 5. Choose Manual Tracking if…

* You prefer **zero reliance on technology** and fear your data being stored on external servers.  
* Your transaction volume is **very low** (just a few expenses a month) and the manual effort isn’t burdensome.  
* You have **limited internet access** or live in areas with long‑term connectivity issues.  
* You enjoy **customizing** every detail of a spreadsheet, crafting specific formulas that no app offers.  
* Even a small subscription feels like a “coffee a day” you’d rather save.

**Practical example:** *Carlos*, a retiree, runs a fixed budget with few monthly changes. He uses a simple notebook to jot down essential expenses and reviews the total each quarter. The manual method gives him a feeling of total control and zero risk of data leakage.

## 6. How to migrate pain‑free

If you’ve decided to give FinMoovi a try but have a history of paper receipts or spreadsheets, follow this **30‑minute** step‑by‑step:

![Migration guide](/images/posts/finmoovi-vs-minhas-economias-qual-vale-mais-6.webp)

1. **Export your spreadsheet** – open the file and save it as CSV (comma‑separated values).  
2. **Import into FinMoovi** – in the app go to *Settings > Import Data* and select the CSV. The algorithm will try to map columns to categories.  
3. **Review categories** – adjust any that don’t fit; the app suggests new categories based on store names.  
4. **Enable smart capture** – take a photo of a recent receipt; confirm the automatic classification is correct.  
5. **Set goals** – pick up to three monthly goals (e.g., “save the equivalent of two meals out”).  
6. **Schedule alerts** – set reminders for recurring bill due dates.

In less than half an hour you’ll have everything synced, watching your cash flow update with every new entry.
