## Why you feel uneasy when a new service asks for your data

You’ve just signed up for a budgeting app, and the first screen asks for your full name, address, a photo of your ID, and even a selfie. Your gut says “that’s a lot,” and you wonder if you’re handing over too much personal info. That uneasy feeling is common: nobody likes to feel exposed, especially when the benefit isn’t clear.

## What "Know Your Customer" really means

"Know Your Customer" (KYC) is a set of rules that financial services use to verify who you are. The goal is to prevent fraud, money laundering, and other illegal activities. In practice, KYC means the provider will:

- Check your identity against official documents (passport, driver’s license, etc.).
- Confirm your address with a utility bill or similar proof.
- Track the source of your money to ensure it isn’t tied to illicit activities.

All of this sounds bureaucratic, but it protects both you and the institution. When the process is clunky, you might abandon the app altogether, missing out on tools that could help you see where your money goes each month.
