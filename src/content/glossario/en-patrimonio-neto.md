## What is Net Worth?

Net Worth = Assets – Liabilities. In other words: everything you have minus everything you owe. It’s the most important number in your [personal finance](/es/posts/es-5-alternativas-a-organizze-en-2026-opciones-para-administrar) — it shows your real wealth.

## How to calculate?

### Your Assets (what you own)

- Money in accounts and investments  
- Real estate ([value](/es/glossario/es-valor-financiero) on the market)  
- Vehicles (market value)  
- Other valuable belongings  

### Your Liabilities (what you owe)

- Real‑estate financing (outstanding balance)  
- Vehicle financing  
- Personal loans  
- Credit card [credit](/es/posts/es-tarjeta-de-credito-vs-tarjeta-de-debito-cual-vale-mas-la-pen) (open statement)  
- Other debts  

### Example

| Assets | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Investments | R$ 50.000 |
| Property | R$ 300.000 |
| Car | R$ 40.000 |
| **Total Assets** | **R$ 390.000** |

| Liabilities | Value |
|-------------|-------|
| Property financing | R$ 200.000 |
| Car financing | R$ 25.000 |
| **Total Liabilities** | **R$ 225.000** |

**Net Worth = R$ 390.000 – R$ 225.000 = R$ 165.000**

## Why track it

- Shows whether you’re getting richer or poorer  
- Helps set concrete goals (e.g., “I want R$ 1 million in net worth”)  
- Reveals if your debts are under control  
- More important than salary (high income with high debt = low net worth)

## Net‑worth goal by age (reference)

- 30 years: 1× your annual salary  
- 40 years: 3× your annual salary  
- 50 years: 6× your annual salary  
- 60 years: 8× your annual salary
