## What is Net Worth?

Net Worth = Assets - Liabilities. In other words: everything you have minus everything you owe. It's the most important number in your [personal finances](/posts/5-alternativas-ao-organizze-em-2026-opcoes-para-gerenciar-su) — shows your real wealth.

## How to calculate

### Your Assets (what you have)

- [Money](/posts/cashback-inteligente) in account and [investments](/posts/cdb-vs-tesouro-selic-qual-rende-mais-em-2026)
- Real estate (market [value](/glossario/valor-financeiro))
- Vehicles (market value)
- Other valuable assets

### Your Liabilities (what you owe)

- [Financing](/glossario/financiamento) for real estate (outstanding balance)
- Vehicle financing
- Personal loans
- [Credit card](/posts/cartao-de-credito-vs-cartao-de-debito-qual-vale-mais-a-pena-) (open bill)
- Other debts

### Example

| Assets | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Investments | R$ 50.000 |
| Property | R$ 300.000 |
| Car | R$ 40.000 |
| **Total Assets** | **R$ 390.000** |

| Liabilities | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Real estate financing | R$ 200.000 |
| Car financing | R$ 25.000 |
| **Total Liabilities** | **R$ 225.000** |

**Net Worth = R$ 390.000 - R$ 225.000 = R$ 165.000**

## Why track it

- Shows whether you are getting richer or poorer  
- Allows you to set concrete goals (e.g., "I want R$ 1 million in net worth")  
- Reveals if your debts are under control  
- It's more important than salary (high income with high debts = low net worth)

## Net worth target by age (reference)

- 30 years: 1× your annual salary  
- 40 years: 3× your annual salary  
- 50 years: 6× your annual salary  
- 60 years: 8× your annual salary
