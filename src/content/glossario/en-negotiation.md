## What is negotiation
Negotiation is the everyday back‑and‑forth you do when buying a sofa, asking for a raise, or trying to lower a [credit](/en/glossario/en-credit)‑card fee. It’s simply the process of reaching an agreement that [benefits](/en/posts/en-cdb-vs-treasury-selic-which-yields-more-in-2026) both sides, even if the benefit isn’t equal. In plain language, it’s the art of getting a better deal without hurting the relationship.

## The pain you probably know
You’ve probably felt that sting: a grocery receipt that’s a few dollars higher than expected, a phone plan that suddenly adds hidden fees, or a [loan](/en/glossario/en-loan) offer that seems too good to be true. Those moments happen because we often accept the first price we see, or we don’t have the right information at hand to argue for a discount. The result? Money that could have been saved or redirected to a vacation fund disappears into “unnecessary” expenses.

## Common negotiation scenarios
- Buying a used car or a piece of furniture  
- Signing a new lease or renewing an existing one  
- Discussing a salary increase or a freelance rate  
- Asking a bank to waive an annual fee  
- Negotiating a discount on a subscription service
