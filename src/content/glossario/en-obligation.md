## The moment you realize your paycheck disappears
You just received your salary, paid the rent that costs about one month’s rent, bought groceries, and maybe treated yourself to a coffee that feels like a small vacation. A week later, you open your bank app and see that a chunk of money has vanished, but you can’t remember where it went. That hidden drain is often an **obligation**—a recurring financial commitment that you may have forgotten or underestimated.
