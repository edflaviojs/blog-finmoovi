## What is
The financial term "value" refers to the price or amount of [dinero](/es/posts/es-ahorra-en-las-compras-del-supermercado) assigned to an asset, such as a stock, a bond, a real estate property, etc. In other words, it is the amount of money someone is willing to pay for something. For example, if you buy a house for R$ 250.000, the value of the house is R$ 250.000.  

The value of an asset can vary over time due to different factors, such as supply and demand, [inflación](/es/glossario/es-inflacion), the [economía](/es/posts/es-5-alternativas-al-mobills-en-2026) in general, etc. For example, if the [economía](/es/posts/es-5-consejos-para-reorganizar-tus-finanzas-en-vacaciones) is in recession, the value of a house may decrease, while if the [economía](/es/posts/es-5-consejos-para-prepararse-financieramente-para-el-segundo-s) is booming, the value of the house may increase.

![Qué es](/images/glossario/valor-inline-1.webp)

## How it works
The value of an asset is determined by the interaction of supply and demand in the market. When there are more buyers than sellers, the asset’s value tends to rise, and when there are more sellers than buyers, the asset’s value tends to fall.  

* The value of an asset can be influenced by factors such as:
  + Inflation: if inflation is high, the value of money decreases, which means the value of an asset can increase.
  + The overall economy: if the economy is booming, the value of an asset can increase, while if the economy is in recession, the asset’s value can decrease.
  + Supply and demand: if there are more buyers than sellers, the asset’s value tends to rise, and when there are more sellers than buyers, the asset’s value tends to fall.

## Advantages
The value of an asset can be a useful tool for making informed financial decisions. For example, if you are considering buying a house, it is important to research the house’s value in the market to ensure you are paying a fair price.  

**Practical tip:** always research the value of an asset before making a financial decision. You can use online tools, such as a finance app, to get information about an asset’s value.

![Ventajas](/images/glossario/valor-inline-3.webp)

## Risks
However, the value of an asset can also be a risk factor. For example, if you buy a house and its value drops, you can lose money.  

* Some of the risks associated with an asset’s value include:
  + Market risk: the value of an asset can decline due to market changes.
  + Inflation risk: inflation can reduce the value of money, meaning the asset’s value can decline.
  + Liquidity risk: if there are not enough buyers for an asset, its value can decrease.  

## Practical examples
For example, suppose you earn R$ 5.000 per month and are considering buying a house that costs R$ 200.000. If the house’s value increases by 10% in a year, the house’s value will be R$ 220.000.  

**Practical tip:** always consider an asset’s value relative to your income and expenses. In this case, if your salary is R$ 5.000 per month, it is important to consider whether you can afford the mortgage and other associated costs.  

Another example is if you own a stock that costs R$ 100 and the stock’s value rises by 20% in a month. In this case, the stock’s value would be R$ 120.  

**Practical tip:** always diversify your investments to minimize risk. In this case, if you have a stock that costs R$ 100, it is important to consider investing in other assets, such as bonds or mutual funds, to minimize risk.
