## What is [Valor](/glossario/valor-financeiro) Present?

Present Value is how much a sum of money that you will receive in the FUTURE is worth TODAY. R$ 1.000 in one year is worth less than R$ 1.000 today — because today you could invest it and have more in the future.

## Formula

**VP = VF ÷ (1 + i)^n**

Where:
- VP = Present Value
- VF = Future Value
- i = [taxa](/glossario/taxa-financeira) de [juros](/glossario/juros)
- n = number of periods

## Practical example

Someone offers you R$ 10.000 in 2 years. With the [Selic](/glossario/selic) at 13% per year, how much is that worth today?

VP = 10.000 ÷ (1,13)² = 10.000 ÷ 1,2769 = **R$ 7.831**

In other words: receiving R$ 10.000 in 2 years is the same as receiving R$ 7.831 today.

## What it is used for

1. **Compare investments** with different time horizons  
2. **Evaluate proposals** ("pay R$ 50.000 upfront or R$ 60.000 in 12x?")  
3. **Price securities** of [renda fixa](/glossario/renda-fixa)  
4. **Business decisions** (is this project worthwhile?)

## [Aplicação](/glossario/aplicacao-financeira) in daily life

When a store offers "12x interest‑free of R$ 100 or R$ 1.000 cash":
- 12 installments = R$ 1.200 in total  
- But the present value of 12 × R$ 100 is less than R$ 1.200  
- If you can invest the money at 1%/month, paying in installments and investing the difference is better

## Simple rule

Money today is worth more than money tomorrow. Always consider the “time cost” when making financial decisions.
