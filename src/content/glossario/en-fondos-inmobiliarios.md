## What are Real Estate Funds (FIIs)?

Real Estate Funds are like “investor condominiums” that pool [money](/es/posts/es-ahorra-en-las-compras-del-supermercado) to buy properties (shopping centers, warehouses, offices) or real‑estate sector securities. You buy shares on the stock exchange and receive monthly rents.

## How does it work?

1. The fund buys properties with the [money](/es/posts/es-ahorrando-en-la-cuenta-de-energia) of the investors  
2. The properties are rented to companies  
3. The rents are distributed monthly to the investors  
4. Returns are **exempt from IR** for individuals  

## Advantages

- Monthly income exempt from tax  
- [Invest in real estate](/es/posts/es-guia-completa-de-fondos-inmobiliarios-para-principiantes) with little [money](/es/posts/es-ahorrando-en-la-cuenta-del-mercado) (starting from R$ 10)  
- [Diversification](/es/posts/es-etfs-que-son-y-como-invertir-de-forma-simple-y-rentable) (a fund can own several properties)  
- [Liquidity](/es/glossario/es-liquidez) (sell on the stock exchange at any time)  
- No need to deal with tenants  

## Types of FIIs

| Type   | Example | Characteristic                     |
|--------|---------|------------------------------------|
| Brick  | HGLG11  | Physical properties (warehouses)  |
| Paper  | KNCR11  | Real‑estate debt securities        |
| Hybrid | KNRI11  | Mix of brick and paper             |
| FOF    | BCFF11  | Fund that invests in other FIIs    |

## How much does it yield?

The market average is 0.7% to 1% per month in returns. With R$ 100,000 invested, you can receive R$ 700‑1,000/month exempt from IR.
