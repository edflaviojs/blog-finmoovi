## What is Financing?

Financing is when a bank lends [money](/es/posts/es-ahorra-en-las-compras-del-supermercado) so you can buy something of high [value](/es/glossario/es-valor-financiero) (house, car, etc.) and you pay it back in installments over years. The purchased asset remains as collateral — if you stop paying, the bank can take it.

## Common types

### Real estate financing

- Term: up to 35 years
- Rates: 8% to 12% per year
- Minimum down payment: 20% of the price
- Systems: SAC or Price

### Vehicle financing

- Term: up to 60 months
- Rates: 1.5% to 3% per month
- Down payment: the more, the better
- Car remains pledged to the bank

## [Tips](/es/posts/es-7-errores-financieros-principiante) for financing well

1. **Give the largest down payment possible** — reduces total interest  
2. **Choose the shortest term you can afford** — less interest  
3. **Compare rates between banks** — the difference can be huge  
4. **Prefer SAC** — pay less total interest  
5. **Make extra payments whenever you can** — every extra R$ 1.000 saves thousands  

## How much does financing cost

A property of R$ 400.000 financed over 30 years at 10% a year costs in total about R$ 1.000.000. You pay more than double the property's price in interest.
