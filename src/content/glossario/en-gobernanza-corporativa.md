## What is governance
Governance refers to the way an organization or company is directed and managed, covering aspects such as decision‑making, supervision, and control. In the context of [personal finance](/es/posts/es-5-alternativas-a-organizze-en-2026-opciones-para-administrar), governance applies to how we manage our own resources and make financial decisions. This includes planning, the [budget](/es/posts/es-5-consejos-para-reorganizar-tus-finanzas-en-vacaciones), investing and risk management. Effective financial governance is crucial to achieve long‑term stability and financial security.

Financial governance involves setting clear, measurable goals, such as saving for a home, paying off debts, or investing in your children’s education. It also means continuously monitoring and adjusting our finances to ensure we are on the right track to achieve those goals. 

![What is governance](/images/glossario/governanca-inline-1.webp)



## How it works
Financial governance works by implementing a set of practices and habits that help us manage our [money](/es/posts/es-ahorrando-en-la-cuenta-de-energia) better. This can include:
* Create a budget that reflects our income and real [expenses](/es/posts/es-5-consejos-para-prepararse-financieramente-para-el-segundo-s)
* Set short‑term and long‑term financial goals
* Implement a [savings](/es/posts/es-ahorra-en-las-compras-del-supermercado) and investment plan
* Monitor and control expenses
* Periodically review and adjust our financial strategies

For example, if you earn R$5.000 per month and want to save R$1.000 for an [emergency fund](/es/glossario/es-fondo-de-emergencia), you should plan your expenses so you can reach this goal. **Practical tip:** Use the 50/30/20 rule, where 50% of your income goes to basic expenses like rent and food, 30% to discretionary spending like entertainment, and 20% to savings and investment.

## Advantages
Financial governance offers numerous advantages, including:
* Greater control over your finances
* Reduced financial stress
* Improved ability to save and invest
* Greater long‑term financial security
* Ability to achieve personal financial goals, such as buying a home or retiring early

With better control over your finances, you can make informed decisions about how to spend and save your money. **Practical tip:** Review your bank statements and credit card statements regularly to identify areas where you can cut expenses and increase your savings.

![Advantages](/images/glossario/governanca-inline-3.webp)



## Risks
Although financial governance is crucial for financial success, there are also risks associated with poor financial management, such as:
* Excessive debt
* Lack of emergency savings
* Investing in high‑risk assets without proper research
* Not diversifying investments
* Not having a retirement plan

It is important to be aware of these risks and take steps to mitigate them. **Practical tip:** Diversify your investments to reduce risk, and consider consulting a financial advisor for personalized guidance.
