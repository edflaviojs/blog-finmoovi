## What is

Application is the name we give to the act of putting your [dinheiro](/posts/cashback-inteligente) into an investment, be it a CDB, investment fund, [Tesouro Direto](/glossario/tesouro-direto) or even a savings account. In simple terms, it’s “lending” your money so it earns more than it would just sit in a checking account. When someone says “make an application”, they are talking about allocating financial resources with the aim of obtaining a return.

![O que é](/images/glossario/aplicacao-inline-1.webp)

## How it works

The logic behind any application is the same: you deliver an amount today and, in return, receive that amount back plus interest or earnings. Each type of application has a term (the time the money stays “locked”) and a rate (how much you will earn).

- **Term:** can be short (30 days), medium (1 to 3 years) or long (5 years or more).  
- **Rate:** can be fixed (the same percentage throughout the whole period) or variable (changes according to the [economia](/posts/5-alternativas-ao-mobills-em-2026)).  
- **Yield:** the gain you obtain, which can be expressed as an annual percentage (for example, 8% a.a.) or in gross amount.

## Advantages

Investing your money brings benefits that go far beyond “seeing the account grow”.

![Vantagens](/images/glossario/aplicacao-inline-3.webp)

- **Safety:** some products, like Tesouro Direto, are guaranteed by the government.  
- **Return:** most applications yield more than the savings account, which today pays less than 0.5% per month.  
- **[Diversificação](/glossario/diversificacao):** by spreading resources across different types of application, you reduce the total risk of your portfolio.  
- **Liquidity:** some applications allow you to redeem the money quickly, such as [renda fixa](/glossario/renda-fixa) funds with daily liquidity.

## Risks

No investment is 100% risk‑free. Knowing the main dangers helps you choose the right application for your profile.

- **Credit risk:** if the institution that issued the security goes bankrupt, you may lose part or all of the capital.  
- **Market risk:** products linked to indexes (like stocks or equity funds) can fluctuate a lot, making your money worth less in certain periods.  
- **Liquidity risk:** some investments can only be redeemed after a defined term, which can interfere with financial emergencies.  
- **Inflation risk:** if the application yields less than inflation, your purchasing power decreases over time.

## Practical examples

Imagine you earn R$4.500 per month and can set aside 10% (R$450) to invest. See how different applications can transform that amount over 2 years:

- **CDB with a rate of 110% of CDI (approx. 6.5% a.a.):** R$450 monthly invested would yield about R$12.200 at the end of 24 months.  
- **Tesouro Selic (yield close to the Selic rate, 13.75% a.a.):** The same contribution would result in roughly R$12.800, with high safety.  
- **Fixed‑income fund with an 8% a.a. rate:** The amount would be around R$11.900, offering good liquidity and professional management.

If the salary were R$7.200 and the reserve 15% (R$1.080), the numbers rise proportionally, showing how the discipline of investing regularly can generate a significant “extra money” for your dream trip or a down‑payment on a property.

## How to start

Taking the first step may seem complicated, but just follow a few simple steps:

1. **Define your goal:** car purchase, [reserva de emergência](/glossario/reserva-de-emergencia) or retirement.  
2. **Build a budget:** identify how much is left after fixed and variable expenses.  
3. **Choose the application:** for beginners, medium‑size bank CDBs or Tesouro Selic are good options.  
4. **Open an account:** use an investment app (like Nubank or Banco Inter) – opening is usually quick and hassle‑free.  
5. **Invest the amount:** schedule automatic monthly transfers to ensure discipline.  
6. **Monitor performance:** review your portfolio every 6 months and adjust as income or goals change.

**Practical tip:** before investing, run a simulation of the yield using the calculator built into the app itself; this helps visualize future gains.  
**Practical tip:** keep at least 6 months of expenses in a daily‑liquidity fund for emergencies, avoiding the need to redeem long‑term investments early.  
**Practical tip:** diversify at least 20% of your capital across different products (CDB, Tesouro and fund), reducing exposure to a single risk.

## Start today

Don’t wait for the next paycheck to put your money to work. Choose a goal, open your investment app and make the first application today. Every real invested now is one step closer to the financial freedom you deserve.
