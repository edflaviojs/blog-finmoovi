## What is Financing?

Financing is when a bank lends [dinheiro](/posts/cashback-inteligente) to you to buy something of [valor](/glossario/valor-financeiro) high (house, car, etc.) and you pay it back in installments over years. The purchased asset remains as collateral — if you stop paying, the bank can take it.

## Common types

### Mortgage financing

- Term: up to 35 years
- Rates: 8% to 12% per year
- Minimum down payment: 20% of the price
- Systems: SAC or Price

### Vehicle financing

- Term: up to 60 months
- Rates: 1.5% to 3% per month
- Down payment: the more, the better
- Car remains pledged to the bank

## [Tips](/posts/7-erros-financeiros-iniciante) for financing well

1. **Put down the largest possible down payment** — reduces total [juros](/glossario/juros)  
2. **Choose the shortest term you can afford** — less interest  
3. **Compare rates across banks** — the difference can be huge  
4. **Prefer SAC** — pays less total interest  
5. **Amortize whenever you can** — each extra R$ 1.000 saves thousands  

## How much does financing cost

A property priced at R$ 400.000 financed over 30 years at 10% a.a. costs in total about R$ 1.000.000. You end up paying more than double the property's price in interest.
