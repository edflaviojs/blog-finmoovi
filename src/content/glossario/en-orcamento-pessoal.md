## What is [Personal Budget](/posts/5-dicas-para-reorganizar-suas-financas-nas-ferias)?

A personal budget is a plan that shows how much [money](/posts/cashback-inteligente) comes in, how much goes out, and where it goes. It’s the most basic and powerful tool of [personal finance](/posts/5-alternativas-ao-organizze-em-2026-opcoes-para-gerenciar-su). Without a budget, you’re driving in the dark.

## How to create a budget

### Step 1: List all income

- Net salary
- Extra income (freelance, side gigs)
- Returns from [investments](/posts/cdb-vs-tesouro-selic-qual-rende-mais-em-2026)
- Others (rent, alimony)

### Step 2: List all expenses

**Fixed (don’t change):**
- Rent/[financing](/glossario/financiamento)
- Condominium
- Health plan
- Internet/phone

**Variable (change every month):**
- Food
- Transportation
- Leisure
- Shopping

### Step 3: Apply the 50-30-20 rule

- 50% for needs
- 30% for wants
- 20% for financial goals

## Budget tools

- **[FinMoovi](/posts/5-alternativas-ao-mobills-em-2026)** — app with automatic categorization by AI
- Simple spreadsheet (Google Sheets)
- Notebook and pen (works!)

## Common mistakes

1. Not including small expenses (coffee, delivery)
2. Forgetting annual expenses (IPVA, IPTU, insurance)
3. Not reviewing monthly
4. Making the budget too restrictive (unsustainable)
