## What is Financial Independence?

Financial independence is when your [investments](/posts/cdb-vs-tesouro-selic-qual-rende-mais-em-2026) generate enough income to pay all your bills — without having to work. You work because you want to, not because you need to.

## The formula

**Required wealth = Annual [Expenses](/posts/5-dicas-para-se-preparar-financeiramente-para-o-segundo-seme) × 25**

This is based on the "4% rule": you can withdraw 4% of the wealth per year without depleting it.

## Example

- Monthly expenses: R$ 5.000
- Annual expenses: R$ 60.000
- Required wealth: R$ 60.000 × 25 = **R$ 1.500.000**

With R$ 1.5 million invested earning 8% per year, you would have R$ 120.000/year (R$ 10.000/month) — more than enough.

## How long it takes

It depends on how much you save:

| [Rate](/glossario/taxa-financeira) of [savings](/glossario/poupanca) | Time to FI |
|-----------------|---------------|
| 10% of income | 51 years |
| 20% of income | 37 years |
| 30% of income | 28 years |
| 50% of income | 17 years |
| 70% of income | 8 years |

## Steps to financial independence

1. Calculate your actual monthly expenses
2. Define your "number" (expenses × 25)
3. Increase your savings rate
4. Invest consistently ([monthly contributions](/ferramentas/simulador-investimento))
5. Reinvest all earnings
6. Increase income (promotions, side hustle)
7. Track progress monthly

## Partial financial independence

It doesn’t have to be all or nothing. Having 50% of your expenses covered by [passive income](/posts/fundos-imobiliarios-para-iniciantes-guia-completo) already changes your life — you can work part‑time, change careers or accept a lower salary doing what you love.
