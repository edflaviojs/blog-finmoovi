## What is Xetra
Xetra is the electronic trading platform operated by the Frankfurt Stock Exchange. It lets you buy and sell shares, ETFs, and bonds in real time, using a computer instead of a noisy trading floor. Think of it as the online checkout lane for the world’s biggest companies – you click, the order is matched, and the trade is recorded instantly. Because it’s fully automated, prices are transparent and you can trade from your laptop or phone whenever the market is open.
