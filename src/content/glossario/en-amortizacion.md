## What is Amortization?

Amortization is the act of paying the [valor](/es/glossario/es-valor-financiero) principal of a debt little by little. Each installment you pay has two parts: the amortization (which reduces the outstanding balance) and the interest (which is the cost of the loan).

## Types of amortization

### SAC Table (Constant Amortization System)

- Fixed amortization in all installments
- Installments start higher and decrease
- Pays less interest in total

### Price Table

- Fixed installments from start to finish
- At the beginning, most of the installment is interest
- Pays more interest in total than SAC

## Practical example

[Financiamiento](/es/glossario/es-financiamiento) of R$ 100.000 over 30 years at 10% a.a.:
- **SAC:** first installment ~R$ 1.111, last ~R$ 282. Total paid: ~R$ 250.000
- **Price:** all installments ~R$ 877. Total paid: ~R$ 316.000

## Extraordinary amortization

You can make extra payments to reduce the outstanding balance faster. This saves thousands in interest. Whenever you have [dinero](/es/posts/es-ahorra-en-las-compras-del-supermercado) left over, consider amortizing your debts.
