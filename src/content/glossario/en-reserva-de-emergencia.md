## What is Emergency Reserve?

The emergency reserve is a financial cushion that protects you from unexpected events. It’s the money you can access quickly when something unforeseen happens.

## How much to save?

The recommendation is to have between **3 to 6 months** of your monthly expenses. If you spend R$ 3,000/month, your ideal reserve is between R$ 9,000 and R$ 18,000.

## Where to keep it?

The reserve needs to have **[liquidez](/glossario/liquidez) daily** (immediate access):
- Tesouro [Selic](/glossario/selic)
- [CDB](/glossario/cdb) with daily liquidity (100% CDI or more)
- Interest‑bearing account

**Never** keep it in [ações](/glossario/acoes), [fundos imobiliários](/glossario/fundos-imobiliarios) or investments with a redemption period.

## How to build it?

1. Set the target [valor](/glossario/valor-financeiro) (3-6 months of expenses)
2. Set aside a fixed amount each month (10-20% of income)
3. Automate the transfer
4. Do not touch it until you reach the target amount
