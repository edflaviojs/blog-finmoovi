## What is Financial Independence?

Financial independence is when your investments generate enough income to pay all your bills — without needing to work. You work because you want to, not because you have to.

## The formula

**Required net worth = [Expenses](/es/posts/es-5-consejos-para-prepararse-financieramente-para-el-segundo-s) annual × 25**

## Example

- Monthly expenses: R$ 5.000  
- Annual expenses: R$ 60.000  
- Required net worth: R$ 60.000 × 25 = **R$ 1.500.000**

With R$ 1.5 million invested earning 8% per year, you would have R$ 120.000/year (R$ 10.000/month) — more than enough.

## How long does it take?

It depends on how much you save:

| [Savings](/es/glossario/es-tasa-financiera) [rate](/es/glossario/es-poupanca) | Time to FI |
|-----------------|---------------|
| 10% of income | 51 years |
| 20% of income | 37 years |
| 30% of income | 28 years |
| 50% of income | 17 years |
| 70% of income | 8 years |

## Steps to financial independence

1. Calculate your real monthly expenses  
2. Define your “number” (expenses × 25)  
3. Increase your savings rate  
4. Invest consistently (monthly contributions)  
5. Reinvest all earnings  
6. Increase income (raises, extra earnings)  
7. Track progress monthly  

## Partial financial independence

It doesn’t have to be all or nothing. Having 50% of your expenses covered by passive income already changes your life — you can work part‑time, switch careers, or accept a lower salary doing what you love.
