## What are Dividends?

Dividends are the portion of profit that a company distributes to its shareholders. If you own [ações](/glossario/acoes) of a company that pays dividends, you receive [money](/posts/cashback-inteligente) periodically without having to sell your shares.

## How it works

1. The company makes a profit in the quarter/year  
2. The board decides to distribute part of that profit  
3. Anyone who owned shares on the “record date” receives the dividends  
4. The [money](/posts/como-criar-uma-reserva-de-emergencia) goes straight to your brokerage account  

## Dividends in Brazil

- They are **exempt from [Imposto de Renda](/glossario/imposto-de-renda)** for individuals  
- Brazilian companies are required to distribute at least 25% of profit  
- Sectors that pay the most: banks, utilities, telecommunications  

## Practical example

If you have 100 shares of a company that pays R$ 2.00 per share in dividends:  
- You receive: 100 × R$ 2.00 = **R$ 200,00**  
- Without selling any shares  
- Without paying tax  

## Dividend strategy

Many investors build portfolios focused on dividends to generate monthly [renda passiva](/posts/fundos-imobiliarios-para-iniciantes-guia-completo). With R$ 500,000 invested in solid dividend payers, it’s possible to receive R$ 3,000–5,000 per month.
