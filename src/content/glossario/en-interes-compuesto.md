## What are [Interest](/es/glossario/es-juros) compounds?

Compound interest is interest calculated on the original [value](/es/glossario/es-valor-financiero) **plus** the interest already accumulated. Unlike simple interest (which applies only to the initial amount), compound interest grows exponentially.

## Practical example

R$ 10.000 invested at 10% per year:

| Year | Simple Interest | Compound Interest |
|------|-----------------|--------------------|
| 1 | R$ 11.000 | R$ 11.000 |
| 5 | R$ 15.000 | R$ 16.105 |
| 10 | R$ 20.000 | R$ 25.937 |
| 20 | R$ 30.000 | R$ 67.275 |
| 30 | R$ 40.000 | R$ 174.494 |

## The Rule of 72

To know in how many years your money doubles, divide 72 by the interest [rate](/es/glossario/es-tasa-financiera):
- 12% per year → 72 ÷ 12 = **6 years** to double
- 8% per year → 72 ÷ 8 = **9 years** to double

## Why starting early matters

Someone who invests R$ 500/month from age 25 to 65 (at 10% a year) accumulates **R$ 3,2 million**. Someone who starts at 35 accumulates R$ 1,1 million. A 10‑year difference = 3× less money.
