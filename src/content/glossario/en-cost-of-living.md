## What is the cost of living?
The cost of living is the total amount you need to cover everything that keeps your daily routine running – from the rent of a modest apartment to the price of a daily coffee, from the bus ticket you take to work to the streaming subscription you binge on weekends. It’s not just a number you see on a report; it’s the sum of all the little expenses that, together, determine whether you can comfortably pay the bills, save a little, and still enjoy a night out.

## Why the cost of living feels out of control
Most people notice the problem when the paycheck arrives and the money disappears faster than expected. You might think you have a solid budget, but then an unexpected medical bill, a rise in grocery prices, or a weekend getaway throws everything off balance. The pain point is clear: you cannot see the whole picture, so you keep guessing how much you really spend each month.

## Common pitfalls that hide the real cost
- **Relying on memory** – trying to recall every coffee, snack, or impulse purchase at the end of the month.  
- **Mixing currencies** – traveling or shopping online often means paying in different currencies, which makes tracking harder.  
- **Ignoring small recurring charges** – subscriptions, app fees, or “free” trials that silently drain your account.  
- **Treating credit‑card bills as extra money** – paying only the minimum and forgetting the interest that adds up.
