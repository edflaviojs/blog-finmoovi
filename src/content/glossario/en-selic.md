## What it is
The **central bank base rate** is a fundamental concept in the world of finance. It is the basic **[interest](/en/glossario/en-interest)** rate of the economy, set by the Monetary Policy Committee of the central bank. In other words, it is the interest rate that banks pay to lend money to the government and, in turn, influences the interest rates of all other loans and investments in the country. For example, if you earn $1,000 a month and are thinking about investing in a fixed‑[income](/en/posts/en-building-a-diversified-portfolio-with-r-500) fund, the central bank base rate can directly affect the return on your investment.

* The central bank base rate is used as a reference to calculate the interest rates of loans and investments  
* It is set by the Monetary Policy Committee of the central bank  
* The central bank base rate can influence [inflation](/en/glossario/en-inflation) and the economy’s growth  

![What it is](/images/glossario/selic-inline-1.webp)

The central bank base rate is important because it helps control inflation and maintain economic stability. When the rate is high, loans become more expensive and people tend to save more and spend less, which can help reduce inflation. Conversely, when the rate is low, loans become cheaper and people tend to spend more, which can stimulate economic growth.

## How it works
The central bank base rate is set by the Monetary Policy Committee of the central bank, which meets regularly to discuss monetary policy. The committee considers factors such as inflation, economic growth, and unemployment when setting the rate. When the rate changes, it can affect the interest on loans and investments throughout the country. For example, if the rate is reduced, the interest on loans such as personal [credit](/en/glossario/en-credit) and mortgage [financing](/en/glossario/en-financing) can decrease, making them more accessible to people.

**Practical tip:** It’s important to understand how the central bank base rate affects your investments and loans so you can make informed decisions about your money. For instance, if you have a variable‑interest loan, a reduction in the rate can lower the interest you pay.

The rate can also influence the **value** of the local currency against other currencies. When the rate is high, the local currency may appreciate relative to other currencies, which can affect the country’s exports and imports. For example, if you are an exporter of products, a high rate can make your goods more expensive for foreign buyers, potentially reducing sales.

## Advantages
The central bank base rate offers several advantages for the economy. One of the main advantages is that it helps control inflation. When the rate is high, loans become more expensive and people tend to save more and spend less, which can help curb inflation. In addition, the rate can help maintain economic stability, which is important for attracting [foreign investment](/en/posts/en-investing-in-dollar-in-brazil).

* The rate helps control inflation  
* It can maintain economic stability  
* The rate can influence economic growth

**Practical tip:** Understanding how the rate affects inflation and economic growth is essential for making informed decisions about your investments and loans. For example, if you are considering investing in a fixed‑income fund, a high rate may mean that the fund’s interest payments will be higher.

The rate can also influence the labor market. When the rate is low, loans become cheaper and companies can invest more in expansion and hiring, which can reduce unemployment. For example, if you are a business owner earning $1,600 a month and are thinking about expanding your business, a low rate can make loans more accessible and affordable.

## Risks
The central bank base rate also carries risks. One of the main disadvantages is that it can negatively affect economic growth. When the rate is high, loans become more expensive and people tend to save more and spend less, which can slow growth. Moreover, the rate can influence the value of the local currency against other currencies, affecting exports and imports.

* The rate can negatively affect economic growth  
* It can influence the value of the local currency against other currencies  
* The rate can affect the labor market  

**Practical tip:** It’s important to understand how the rate impacts economic growth and the labor market so you can make informed decisions about your investments and loans. For example, if you are an investor earning $600 a month and are thinking about investing in an equity fund, a high rate may mean higher interest paid by the fund, but it can also increase the risk of loss in the investment’s value.

The rate can also influence investor confidence. When the rate is high, investors may lose confidence and reduce their investments, which can negatively affect economic growth. For instance, if you are a foreign investor earning $2,000 a month and are considering investing abroad, a high rate can make the destination less attractive for investment.
