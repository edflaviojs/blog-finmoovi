## What is
The term "value" is fundamental in the world of finance, but what does it really mean? In summary, value refers to how much something is worth, whether it's an [investment](/posts/building-diversified-portfolio-with-r-500), a good, a service, or even your time. In the financial context, value is determined by a combination of factors, including supply and demand, risk involved, and expected return. For example, if you have an apartment that can be rented for R$ 2,000 per month, the value of that apartment can be estimated based on the income it generates.

## 
To better understand how value works, let's consider some practical examples. If you earn R$ 5,000 per month and spend R$ 3,000 on fixed expenses, the value of your time can be calculated based on your ability to generate income. If you decide to invest R$ 1,000 in a [financial application](/glossario/aplicacao-financeira) that yields 10% per year, the value of that investment will increase over time. Here are some important points to consider:
* Value can vary over time due to changes in the market or economy.
* The value of an investment or asset can be influenced by factors such as [inflation](/glossario/inflacao), [interest rates](/glossario/juros), and exchange rates.
* It's essential to consider the risk involved in determining the value of an investment or asset.

## Advantages
The advantages of understanding and managing the value of your investments and assets are numerous. **Practical tip:** when investing, it's essential to diversify your portfolio to minimize risk and maximize returns. Additionally, it's crucial to have a clear vision of your financial goals and investment timeframe. Here are some advantages of managing the value of your investments:
* Greater control over your finances
* Possibility of increasing wealth over time
* Reduction of the risk of loss of value due to market changes
* Greater flexibility to make informed financial decisions

## Risks
However, it's essential to be aware of the risks involved in investing and managing the value of your assets. **Practical tip:** it's crucial to conduct a thorough analysis of the market and economic conditions before making any investment decisions. Additionally, it's essential to consider your risk tolerance and investment timeframe. Here are some risks to consider:
* Loss of value due to market or economic changes
* Risk of inflation, which can reduce the real value of your investments
* Risk of interest rates, which can increase the cost of loans or reduce the value of your investments
* Risk of [liquidity](/glossario/liquidez), which can make it difficult to sell assets or investments
