## What is Financial Emergency?

Financial emergency is any unexpected situation that demands [money](/posts/cashback-inteligente) that you did not plan to spend. It can be a job loss, a health problem, a car repair or a broken appliance.

## Common Examples

- Job loss (need 3-6 months of expenses)
- Medical emergency (surgery, treatment)
- Car repair (R$ 2.000-5.000)
- Broken appliance (refrigerator, washing machine)
- Problem with the property (leak, plumbing)
- Unexpected fine or tax

## How to Prepare

The best protection against financial emergencies is the **[emergency fund](/glossario/reserva-de-emergencia)**:
- 6 months of expenses for CLT
- 12 months for freelancers/PJ
- In an investment with daily [liquidity](/glossario/liquidez) (Tesouro Selic, [CDB](/glossario/cdb))

## What to Do if You Don't Have an Emergency Fund

If the emergency arrives and you don't have an emergency fund:

1. **Don't use [overdraft](/glossario/cheque-especial)** ([interest](/glossario/juros) of 15%/month)
2. **Don't use credit card revolving credit** (interest of 16%/month)
3. Request a consigned loan (interest of 1-2%/month)
4. Anticipate FGTS or 13th salary
5. Sell something you don't use (marketplace)
6. Negotiate direct installment payment with the creditor

## Prevention

After resolving the emergency, prioritize building your emergency fund. Start with R$ 1.000 (already covers most small unforeseen expenses) and increase it up to 6 months of expenses.
