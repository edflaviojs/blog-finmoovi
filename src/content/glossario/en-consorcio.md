## What is a Consortium?

Consortium is a system where several people join together to buy something (car, property, motorcycle). Every month, each participant pays an installment. Each month, one or more participants are awarded (by lottery or bid) and receive a credit letter to purchase the asset.

## How it works

1. You join a consortium group  
2. Pay monthly installments (without [interest](/glossario/juros), but with an administration [fee](/glossario/taxa-financeira))  
3. Every month there is an assembly with a lottery  
4. Awarded participants receive a credit letter  
5. You can place a bid (offer advanced installments) to be awarded earlier  

## Advantages

- No interest (only administration fee of 10-20% of the total)  
- Forced [savings](/glossario/poupanca) discipline  
- Cash purchase power (credit letter)  
- Can use FGTS for a bid (real estate)  

## Disadvantages

- You don't know when you'll be awarded  
- Administration fee can be high  
- [Money](/posts/cashback-inteligente) stays locked until awarded or the group ends  
- Installments may be adjusted  

## Consortium vs. [Financing](/glossario/financiamento)

| | Consortium | Financing |
|--|-----------|---------------|
| Interest | None | 8-15% per year |
| Receives the asset | When awarded | Immediately |
| Total cost | Lower | Much higher |
| Predictability | Low | High |

## When it's worth it

- When you aren't in a hurry to receive the asset  
- To swap a car in 2-3 years  
- As a form of forced savings  
- When you want to avoid financing interest
