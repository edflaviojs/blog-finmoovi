## What is inflation?

Inflation is the general rise in prices. When inflation goes up, the same money buys fewer things. That's why R$ 100 today buys less than R$ 100 ten years ago.

## How is it measured in Brazil?

The main index is the **IPCA** (Índice de Precios al Consumidor Amplio), calculated by IBGE. It measures the price variation of a basket of products and services consumed by families.

## Why does it matter for your finances?

If your investments earn less than inflation, you are **losing money** in real terms. Example:
- Inflation: 5 % per year
- [Poupança](/es/glossario/es-poupanca): 6 % per year
- Real gain: only 1 %

## How to protect yourself?

- Invest in assets that yield above inflation
- IPCA+ Treasury guarantees [rendimiento](/es/posts/es-montando-cartera-diversificada-con-r-500) real
- Diversify between [renda fixa](/es/glossario/es-renda-fixa) and variable
- Follow the monthly IPCA
