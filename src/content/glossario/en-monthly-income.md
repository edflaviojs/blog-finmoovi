## What is monthly income?
Many people feel a knot in the stomach every time the paycheck lands: “Will this be enough to cover the rent, the groceries, and the occasional outing?” That uneasy feeling is the core of **monthly income** – the total amount of money you receive regularly, whether it comes from a salary, freelance work, pensions, or side‑hustles. It’s the baseline you use to decide how much you can spend, save, or invest each month.

## Why tracking monthly income matters
When you know exactly how much money flows in, you can stop guessing and start planning. A clear picture of your income lets you:
- Set realistic budgets without constantly hitting the “out of money” wall.
- Identify periods when extra cash appears (e.g., a bonus) and allocate it wisely.
- Compare income trends over time, spotting growth or warning signs early.

![What is monthly income?](/images/glossario/renda-mensal-inline-1.webp)



Without that clarity, even a modest increase in earnings can feel like a financial roller‑coaster, because you lack a reference point for your expenses.

## Typical pitfalls
Most people stumble over the same three mistakes when handling their monthly income:

- **Ignoring irregular earnings** – Freelance gigs or seasonal work get tossed aside, leading to under‑budgeting.
- **Mixing cash and digital transactions** – Small cash purchases disappear from the mental ledger, skewing the real picture.
- **Waiting for the month‑end to review** – By the time you look at the numbers, it’s too late to adjust spending for that period.
