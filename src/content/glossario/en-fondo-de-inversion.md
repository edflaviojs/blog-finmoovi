## What is
Funds are a form of collective investment that allows investors to combine their resources to invest in a variety of assets, such as stocks, bonds, real estate, etc. This is done through a company that manages the fund, selecting the [investments](/es/posts/es-cdb-vs-tesouro-selic-cual-rinde-mas-en-2026) and distributing the returns among investors. Funds can be of different types, such as equity funds, fixed‑income funds, real‑estate investment funds, among others.

Funds offer an accessible way for people with different income levels to invest in the [financial market](/es/posts/es-cotizaciones-semana-1-julio-2026). For example, if you earn a salary of R$5,000 per month and want to invest R$500, you can join a fund that allows you to diversify your investments without needing a large initial capital.

![Qué es](/images/glossario/fundos-inline-1.webp)

* Equity funds: Invest in company stocks
* Fixed‑income funds: Invest in bonds and other debt instruments
* Real‑estate investment funds: Invest in properties and real‑estate projects

## How it works
Funds work as follows: investors buy shares of the fund, and the [money](/es/posts/es-ahorra-en-las-compras-del-supermercado) is used to invest in a diversified portfolio of assets. The company that manages the fund is responsible for selecting the investments, managing risk, and distributing returns to investors. Returns can come in the form of [dividends](/es/glossario/es-dividendos), interest, or capital gains.

For example, if you invest R$1,000 in an equity fund and the fund generates a 10% annual return, your investment will be worth R$1,100 at the end of the year. **Practical tip:** It is important to research and understand the fund’s investment objectives, as well as the associated costs, before making a decision.

Funds also provide a way to diversify your investments, which can help reduce risk. By investing in a fund, you are investing in a variety of assets, which can help protect your investment in case one of the assets underperforms.

## Advantages
Funds offer several advantages for investors, including:
* Diversification: Funds allow investors to diversify their investments, which can help reduce risk.
* Market access: Funds can provide access to markets and assets that would otherwise be difficult for individual investors to reach.
* Professional management: Funds are managed by experienced professionals who select investments and manage risk.
* Economies of scale: Funds can leverage economies of scale to reduce costs and improve returns.

**Practical tip:** It is important to consider your investment goals and risk tolerance before selecting a fund. For example, if you earn a salary of R$3,000 per month and are saving for an emergency, you may want to invest in a fixed‑income fund that offers a more stable return.

## Risks
Although funds can offer several advantages, they also carry risks. Some of the risks associated with funds include:
* Market risk: Funds can be exposed to market movements, which can affect the [value](/es/glossario/es-valor-financiero) of the investment.
* Credit risk: Funds that invest in bonds and other debt instruments can be exposed to credit risk, which can affect the investment’s value.
* Liquidity risk: Funds may have difficulty selling assets quickly if investors wish to withdraw their money.

**Practical tip:** It is important to read and understand the fund’s prospectus and financial reports before investing. For example, if you are considering investing in an equity fund, it is important to research the fund’s investment strategy and associated costs.

## Practical examples
Funds can be an accessible way for people with different income levels to invest in the financial market. For example:
* If you earn a salary of R$8,000 per month and want to invest R$2,000 in an equity fund, you can join a fund that allows you to diversify your investments and potentially achieve a higher return than a traditional savings account.
* If you earn a salary of R$4,000 per month and want to invest R$1,000 in a fixed‑income fund, you can join a fund that provides a more stable and safe return.
