## What is Overdraft?

Overdraft is a limit of [credit](/glossario/credito) that the bank makes available on your checking account. When your balance goes negative, you automatically use the overdraft — and pay [interest](/glossario/juros) that is extremely high for it.

## Why is it so dangerous

- Interest of **8% to 15% per month** (150%+ per year)  
- It is activated automatically (you don’t even notice)  
- Interest is charged daily on the negative balance  
- Many people use it without knowing they are paying

## Practical example

If you stay R$ 1,000 negative for 30 days with interest of 12% per month:  
- Interest: R$ 120  
- In 6 months without paying: R$ 1,000 becomes R$ 1,973  
- In 12 months: R$ 1,000 becomes R$ 3,896  

## How to avoid

1. **Disable the overdraft** — call the bank and ask to remove it  
2. **Keep a reserve in the account** — at least R$ 500 as a cushion  
3. **Set up alerts** — notification when balance gets low  
4. **If you need credit** — personal loan has interest 5× lower
