## What is Score of [Crédito](/es/posts/es-tarjeta-de-credito-vs-tarjeta-de-debito-cual-vale-mas-la-pen)?

The [crédito](/es/glossario/es-credito) score is a 0‑to‑1000 rating that credit bureaus (Serasa, SPC, Boa Vista) assign to you. This rating tells banks and merchants how likely you are to pay your bills on time.

## Score ranges

| Range | Score | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Very low | 0-300 | High risk of delinquency |
| Low | 301-500 | Considerable risk |
| Good | 501-700 | Moderate risk |
| Very good | 701-1000 | Low risk, good payer |

## What affects your score

**Increases:**
- Paying bills on time
- Having a long history of good payments
- Having accounts in your name (electricity, phone)
- Active Positive Registry

**Decreases:**
- Late payments
- Having a negative record (SPC/Serasa)
- Many credit inquiries in a short period
- Using a large portion of your credit limit

## How to improve your score

1. Pay all bills on time (even the small ones)
2. Activate the Positive Registry in Serasa
3. Negotiate and settle outstanding debts
4. Avoid applying for credit at many places at the same time
5. Keep your registration data up to date

## Why the score matters

With a high score you get:
- Lower [juros](/es/glossario/es-juros) rates on financing
- Higher credit‑card limits
- Easier approval for rentals
- Better terms on loans
