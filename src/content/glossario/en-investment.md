## What is investment
Investimento, or investment, is simply putting money to work now so you can earn more later. Think of it like planting a seed: you water it, give it sunlight, and after some time you harvest fruit. The “fruit” can be extra cash for a vacation, a down‑payment on a house, or just a safety net for emergencies. The key idea is that you don’t spend every cent you earn; instead, you allocate a portion to assets that can grow – like stocks, bonds, or even a small business.

## The everyday pain
You’ve probably felt the sting of an unexpected expense – a broken fridge, a medical bill, or a sudden need to replace a laptop. You scramble, dip into savings, maybe even use a credit card and pay high interest. The stress of not having a buffer makes you wonder why you never seem to get ahead, even though you earn a steady paycheck each month. This cycle repeats because the money you earn is mostly spent on bills, groceries, and the occasional treat, leaving nothing to grow.
