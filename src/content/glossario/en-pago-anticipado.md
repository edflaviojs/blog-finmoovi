## What is Early Payment?

Early payment is paying a debt (financing, loan, [consorcio](/es/glossario/es-consorcio)) before the final term. When you do this, you are entitled to a discount on the [juros](/es/glossario/es-juros) that would be charged in the future. It is a right guaranteed by the Consumer Defense Code.

## Your rights

- You can pay any debt early
- The bank is required to give a proportional discount on interest
- It cannot charge a penalty for early payment
- It can be partial (amortize) or total (pay everything)

## How much you save

Example: financing of R$ 200.000 over 30 years at 10% a.a.
- Total you would pay: ~R$ 632.000
- If you pay in 15 years: ~R$ 387.000
- **Savings: R$ 245.000** (almost the [valor](/es/glossario/es-valor-financiero) of the property!)

## When it's worth paying

✅ Worth it:
- The debt interest is higher than the [rendimiento](/es/posts/es-montando-cartera-diversificada-con-r-500) of your investments
- Real estate financing with high [taxa](/es/glossario/es-tasa-financiera) (>9% a.a.)
- Personal and payroll loans
- When you have idle money earning less

❌ Not worth it:
- The debt interest is lower than the return on your investments
- Financing with a very low rate (<7% a.a.)
- If you will run out of emergency reserve

## How to do it

1. Call the bank or access the app
2. Request an early payment simulation
3. Compare the discount offered
4. If you agree, make the payment
5. Request a written payment receipt
