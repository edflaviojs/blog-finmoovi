## What is Volatility?

Volatility is the intensity with which the price of an [investment](/posts/building-a-diversified-portfolio-with-r-500) goes up and down. A volatile asset can appreciate 5% in one day and fall 3% the next day. The higher the volatility, the higher the risk — but also the higher the potential for gain.

## Examples of Volatility

| Asset | Volatility | Typical Daily Variation |
|-------|-------------|----------------------|
| [Savings Account](/glossario/poupanca) | Almost zero | 0% |
| Tesouro Selic | Very low | 0,03% |
| [Stocks](/glossario/acoes) (Ibovespa) | Medium | 1-2% |
| [Bitcoin](/glossario/bitcoin) | Very high | 3-10% |
| Meme coins | Extreme | 10-50% |

## Is Volatility Bad?

Not necessarily. It depends on your profile:
- **Conservative:** volatility is stress. Prefer [fixed income](/glossario/renda-fixa).
- **Moderate:** accepts some oscillation in exchange for more return.
- **Adventurous:** uses volatility to their advantage (buys on dips).

## How to Deal with Volatility

1. **Have a long-term horizon** — in the short term, everything fluctuates
2. **Diversify** — different assets fluctuate at different times
3. **Don't check every day** — checking prices daily generates anxiety
4. **Invest regularly** — monthly contributions smooth out the average price
5. **Have an [emergency fund](/glossario/reserva-de-emergencia)** — so you don't have to sell at a low price

## Volatility Index

In Brazil, the main indicator is the **VIX** (fear index). When the VIX is high, the market is nervous. When it's low, it's calm.
