## What is Early Repayment?

Early repayment is paying a debt ([financing](/glossario/financiamento), loan, [consortium](/glossario/consorcio)) before the final deadline. When you do this, you are entitled to a discount on the [interest](/glossario/juros) that would be charged in the future. It is a right guaranteed by the Consumer Defense Code.

## Your rights

- You can pay off any debt early
- The bank must give a proportional discount on interest
- It cannot charge a penalty for early repayment
- It can be partial (amortize) or total (pay it all off)

## How much you save

Example: financing of R$ 200,000 over 30 years at 10% per year.
- Total you would pay: ~R$ 632,000
- If you repay in 15 years: ~R$ 387,000
- **Savings: R$ 245,000** (almost the [value](/glossario/valor-financeiro) of the property!)

## When it’s worth repaying early

✅ Worth it:
- Debt interest rates are higher than investment returns
- Real estate financing with a high [rate](/glossario/taxa-financeira) (>9% per year)
- Personal and payroll loans
- When you have idle money earning less

❌ Not worth it:
- Debt interest rates are lower than what your investments earn
- Financing with a very low rate (<7% per year)
- If you will be left without an [emergency reserve](/glossario/reserva-de-emergencia)

## How to do it

1. Call the bank or open the app
2. Request an early repayment simulation
3. Compare the discount offered
4. If you agree, make the payment
5. Request a written proof of repayment
