## The everyday mess that makes you sigh

You’ve just paid the rent, bought groceries, and topped up the car fuel tank. A few minutes later, a notification pops up: “Your credit‑card bill is due tomorrow.” You scramble to find the receipt, open a spreadsheet, and try to remember if you already accounted for the coffee you bought on the way to work. The feeling of not knowing where your money really goes is all too common, and it’s the exact problem that “gestão” – or financial management – aims to solve.

![The everyday mess that makes you sigh](/images/glossario/gestao-inline-1.webp)



## What “gestão” really means

In plain language, gestão is the habit of tracking, organizing, and planning your money so you always know what’s coming in, what’s going out, and what you can still afford. It’s not about complex accounting software or fancy jargon; it’s simply a systematic way to keep an eye on your cash flow, set realistic goals, and avoid nasty surprises at the end of the month.

## Step‑by‑step example: from chaos to control

Imagine you earn about three months’ rent each month. Here’s how you could use FinMoovi to manage that income:

1. **Capture income** – When your salary lands, record it with a voice note (“salary, 3 × rent”). The app tags it as “income” and adds it to the cash‑flow chart.
2. **Snap receipts** – Every grocery trip, coffee stop, or online purchase gets photographed. The AI reads the total, assigns “food” or “entertainment,” and updates the monthly spend.
3. **Set a goal** – You want to save the equivalent of a short vacation (about the cost of a weekend hotel). Enter the goal; FinMoovi shows how much you need to set aside each week.
4. **Monitor credit cards** – Import your card statement; the app highlights upcoming due dates and the exact amount needed to avoid interest.
5. **Use shopping mode** – Before a big supermarket run, add items to the list. As you scan each product’s barcode, the total updates, keeping you within the budget you defined.
6. **Get alerts** – Mid‑month, the app notifies you that your “food” budget is 80 % used. You can decide to cut back on dining out for the rest of the month.

By the end of the cycle, you’ll see exactly where the money went, how close you are to your vacation fund, and whether you need to tweak any category. All of this happens without opening a spreadsheet or digging through piles of paper.
