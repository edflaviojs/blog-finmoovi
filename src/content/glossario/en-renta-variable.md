## What is Variable Income?

Variable Income is everything that does not have a guaranteed return. The price goes up and down according to the market. You can earn a lot — or lose. It includes [stocks](/es/glossario/es-acoes), [real estate funds](/es/glossario/es-fondo-de-inversion) , ETFs, cryptocurrencies and derivatives.

## Difference with [Fixed Income](/es/glossario/es-renda-fixa)

| | Fixed Income | Variable Income |
|--|-----------|----------------|
| Return | Predictable | Unpredictable |
| Risk | Low | Medium to high |
| Potential | Limited | Unlimited |
| Ideal term | Short/medium | Long (5+ years) |
| Example | [CDB](/es/glossario/es-cdb), Treasury | Stocks, FIIs |

## Main variable income investments

1. **Stocks** — portions of companies  
2. **[Real Estate Funds](/es/glossario/es-fondos-inmobiliarios) (FIIs)** — shares of funds that invest in properties  
3. **ETFs** — funds that replicate indexes  
4. **Cryptocurrencies** — decentralized digital assets  
5. **Derivatives** — futures contracts, options (advanced)  

## [Risks](/es/posts/es-cdb-vs-tesouro-selic-cual-rinde-mas-en-2026)

- Capital loss (your investment may be worth less)  
- Volatility (daily fluctuations)  
- Company risk (bankruptcy)  
- Market risk (economic crises)  

## Golden rule

Only invest in variable income money that you won’t need in the next 5 years. And always have your emergency reserve in fixed income before you start.
