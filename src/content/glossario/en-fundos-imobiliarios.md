## What are [Funds](/glossario/fundo-de-investimento) Real Estate (FIIs)?

Real Estate Funds are like “investor condominiums” that pool [money](/posts/cashback-inteligente) to buy properties (shopping malls, warehouses, offices) or securities of the real estate sector. You buy shares on the stock exchange and receive monthly rents.

## How it works

1. The fund buys properties with the [money](/posts/como-criar-uma-reserva-de-emergencia) from the shareholders  
2. The properties are rented to companies  
3. The rents are distributed monthly to the shareholders  
4. Distributions are **tax‑free (IR‑exempt)** for individuals  

## Advantages

- Monthly income exempt from tax  
- Invest in real estate with little [money](/posts/como-evitar-compras-por-impulso-e-proteger-seu-bolso) (starting from R$ 10)  
- [Diversification](/glossario/diversificacao) (a fund can own multiple properties)  
- [Liquidity](/glossario/liquidez) (sell instantly on the exchange)  
- No need to deal with tenants  

## Types of FIIs

| Type   | Example | Characteristic                     |
|--------|---------|------------------------------------|
| Brick  | HGLG11  | Physical properties (warehouses)   |
| Paper  | KNCR11  | Real‑estate debt securities         |
| Hybrid | KNRI11  | Mix of brick and paper             |
| FOF    | BCFF11  | Fund that invests in other FIIs    |

## How much does it yield?

The market average is 0.7% to 1% per month in returns. With R$ 100,000 invested, you could receive R$ 700‑1,000/month tax‑free.
