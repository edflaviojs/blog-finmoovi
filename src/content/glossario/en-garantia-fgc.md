## What is the FGC Guarantee?

The FGC (Credit Guarantee Fund) works like insurance for your bank investments. If the bank fails, the FGC returns your money — up to R$ 250,000 per CPF per financial institution.

## What is covered

- CDB (Certificado de Depósito Bancário)
- [LCI e LCA](/glossario/lci-e-lca)
- [Savings](/glossario/poupanca)
- Checking account
- RDB
- Letters of [exchange](/glossario/cambio)

## What is NOT covered

- Stocks
- Investment funds
- [Debentures](/glossario/debentures)
- [Tesouro Direto](/glossario/tesouro-direto) (has federal government guarantee)
- CRI and CRA
- [Private pension](/glossario/previdencia-privada)

## Limits

- **R$ 250,000** per CPF per institution
- **R$ 1,000,000** total (global cap, resets every 4 years)

## Practical strategy

If you have R$ 500,000 to invest in CDB:
- Put R$ 250,000 in Bank A
- Put R$ 250,000 in Bank B
- Both are 100% covered by the FGC

## Important tip

Smaller banks usually offer higher rates (120‑130% of CDI) precisely because they are less known. With the FGC guarantee, the risk is practically the same as a big bank — but the return is higher.
