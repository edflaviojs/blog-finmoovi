## What is Variable Income?

Variable Income is everything that doesn't have a guaranteed return. The price goes up and down according to the market. You can earn a lot — or lose. Includes [ações](/glossario/acoes), [fundos imobiliários](/glossario/fundos-imobiliarios), ETFs, cryptocurrencies and derivatives.

## Difference to [Renda Fixa](/glossario/renda-fixa)

| | Fixed Income | Variable Income |
|--|--------------|-----------------|
| Return | Predictable | Unpredictable |
| Risk | Low | Medium to high |
| Potential | Limited | Unlimited |
| Ideal term | Short/medium | Long (5+ years) |
| Example | CDB, Treasury | Stocks, FIIs |

## Main variable income investments

1. **Stocks** — pieces of companies
2. **Real Estate Funds (FIIs)** — shares of [fundos](/glossario/fundo-de-investimento) that invest in real estate
3. **ETFs** — funds that replicate indexes
4. **Cryptocurrencies** — decentralized digital assets
5. **Derivatives** — futures contracts, options (advanced)

## Risks

- Capital loss (your investment may be worth less)
- [Volatility](/glossario/volatilidade) (daily fluctuations)
- Company risk (bankruptcy)
- Market risk (economic crises)

## Golden rule

Only invest in variable income money you won't need in the next 5 years. And always have your [reserva de emergência](/glossario/reserva-de-emergencia) in fixed income before you start.
