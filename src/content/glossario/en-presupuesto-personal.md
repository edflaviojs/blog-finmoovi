## What is [Presupuesto](/es/posts/es-orcamento-com-salario-minimo-dicas-praticas) Personal?

Personal budget is a plan that shows how much money comes in, how much goes out, and where it goes. It is the most basic and powerful tool of personal finance. Without a budget, you're driving blind.

## How to set up a budget

### Step 1: List all the sources

- Net salary
- Extra income (freelance, occasional jobs)
- Investment returns
- Others (rent, pension)

### Step 2: List all the expenses

**Fixed (do not change):**
- Rent/mortgage
- Condo fees
- Health plan
- Internet/phone

**Variable (change each month):**
- Food
- Transportation
- Leisure
- Shopping

### Step 3: Apply the 50-30-20 rule

- 50% for needs
- 30% for wants
- 20% for financial goals

## Common mistakes

1. Not including small expenses (coffee, delivery)
2. Forgetting annual expenses (IPVA, IPTU, insurance)
3. Not reviewing monthly
4. Making a budget that’s too restrictive (it doesn’t hold up)
