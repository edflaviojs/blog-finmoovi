## What is patrimônio

Patrimônio is the total of everything you own that has value—cash in the bank, investments, the car you drive, the furniture in your home, and even the money you’ve saved for a vacation. Think of it as the sum of all your assets minus any debts you owe. In everyday language, it’s “what you have” versus “what you owe,” and the difference tells you how financially solid you are.

![What is patrimônio](/images/glossario/patrimonio-inline-1.webp)



## Why it matters to you

Imagine you’re [planning](/en/posts/en-budgeting-with-minimum-wage-practical-tips) a big move, buying a house, or simply wanting to feel secure in case of an unexpected expense. Without a clear picture of your patrimônio, you’re guessing whether you can afford that down‑payment, cover a medical bill, or keep your lifestyle when a job ends. The pain point is the same for most people: they have scattered accounts, receipts in drawers, and no single place that tells them, “You have enough to go ahead,” or “You need to tighten the belt.” That uncertainty can lead to stress, missed opportunities, and costly decisions.

## Common mistakes

Many people think that looking at a single bank balance is enough. In reality, they overlook:

![Common mistakes](/images/glossario/patrimonio-inline-3.webp)



- **Hidden liabilities** such as credit‑card debt or an unpaid loan that erodes the net value.
- **Untracked assets** like a collection of foreign‑currency savings or a side‑hustle income that never appears in the main account.
- **Seasonal [cash flow](/en/glossario/en-cash-flow)** where income spikes in one month and drops in another, making the overall picture look healthier than it really is.

These blind spots keep you from making informed choices about savings, investments, or big purchases.
