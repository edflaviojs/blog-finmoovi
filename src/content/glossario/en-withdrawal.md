## What “withdrawal” really means

In plain language, a withdrawal is any movement of money out of an account. It can happen in several ways:

- **Cash withdrawal** – taking physical money from an ATM or a bank teller.
- **Electronic withdrawal** – using a debit card, online transfer, or payment app to move funds.
- **Investment withdrawal** – pulling money from a savings account, mutual fund, or retirement plan.

Each type has its own quirks. Cash withdrawals may incur fees if you use an out‑of‑network ATM. Electronic withdrawals are usually free but can trigger overdraft charges if you exceed your balance. Investment withdrawals might be taxed or penalized if you pull money before a certain age. Understanding these nuances helps you avoid hidden costs and keep your budget on track.
