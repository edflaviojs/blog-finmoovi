## What is an Emergency Reserve?

An emergency reserve is a financial cushion that protects you from unforeseen events. It’s the money you can access quickly when something unexpected happens.

## How much to save?

The recommendation is to have between **3 to 6 months** of your monthly expenses. If you spend R$ 3,000/month, your ideal reserve is between R$ 9,000 and R$ 18,000.

## Where to keep it?

The reserve needs to have **[liquidity](/es/glossario/es-liquidez) daily** (immediate access):
- Tesouro Selic
- [CDB](/es/glossario/es-cdb) with daily liquidity (100% [CDI](/es/glossario/es-cdi) or more)
- Interest‑bearing account

**Never** leave it in [stocks](/es/glossario/es-acoes), [funds](/es/glossario/es-fondo-de-inversion) real‑estate or investments with a redemption period.

## How to build it?

1. Define the [value](/es/glossario/es-valor-financiero) target (3‑6 months of expenses)
2. Set aside a fixed amount each month (10‑20% of income)
3. Automate the transfer
4. Don’t touch it until you reach the target amount
