## What is Liquidity?

Liquidity is the ability to turn an investment into cash quickly. The more liquid it is, the faster you can access the money.

## Types of liquidity

- **Daily liquidity (D+0):** Money available on the same day. E.g., Tesouro Selic, [CDB](/glossario/cdb) with daily liquidity.
- **Liquidity D+1:** Available the next day. E.g., Some [fundos](/glossario/fundo-de-investimento) investment funds.
- **Liquidity at maturity:** Can only be redeemed on the agreed date. E.g., CDB with term, LCI/LCA.

## Liquidity scale

From most liquid to least liquid:

1. Cash in checking account
2. Tesouro [Selic](/glossario/selic) / CDB daily liquidity
3. DI funds
4. CDB with term
5. [Fundos imobiliários](/glossario/fundos-imobiliarios)
6. [Ações](/glossario/acoes)
7. Real estate

## When does liquidity matter?

- **[Reserva de emergência](/glossario/reserva-de-emergencia):** needs daily liquidity
- **Short‑term goals (< 1 year):** high liquidity
- **Long‑term goals (> 5 years):** can forego liquidity for higher returns
