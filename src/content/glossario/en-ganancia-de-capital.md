## What is Capital Gain?

Capital gain is the profit you get when you sell something for more than you paid for it. You bought a stock for R$ 20 and sold it for R$ 30? Your capital gain is R$ 10. This gain can be taxed by the Income Tax.

## Where it applies

- Sale of stocks (more than R$ 20,000/month)
- Sale of real estate
- Sale of cryptocurrencies (more than R$ 35,000/month)
- Sale of vehicles with profit
- Sale of any asset above the [value](/es/glossario/es-valor-financiero) of purchase

## Rates

### Stocks

- Regular transactions: 15% on the gain
- Day trade: 20% on the gain
- Exemption: sales up to R$ 20,000/month

### Real estate

- 15% on the capital gain
- Exemption: single property up to R$ 440,000 (if you haven’t sold another in the last 5 years)
- Exemption: if you use the amount to buy another property within 180 days

### Cryptocurrencies

- 15% on the gain for sales over R$ 35,000/month

## How to calculate?

**Capital Gain = Sale Price – Purchase Price – [Costs](/es/posts/es-prevision-privada-2026-vale-la-pena-guia-practica)**

Costs include: brokerage fees, taxes, renovations (real estate), etc.

## Important tip

Keep a record of all purchases (brokerage statements, contracts). Without proof of the purchase price, the tax authority can consider the cost to be zero — and charge tax on the total sale amount.
