## What is Inflation?

Inflation is the general rise in prices. When inflation goes up, the same [dinheiro](/posts/cashback-inteligente) buys fewer things. That's why R$ 100 today buys less than R$ 100 ten years ago.

## How is it measured in Brazil?

The main index is the **[IPCA](/glossario/ipca)** (Broad Consumer Price Index), calculated by IBGE. It measures the price variation of a basket of products and services consumed by households.

## Why does it matter for your finances?

If your [investimentos](/posts/cdb-vs-tesouro-selic-qual-rende-mais-em-2026) earn less than inflation, you are **losing [dinheiro](/posts/como-criar-uma-reserva-de-emergencia)** in real terms. Example:
- Inflation: 5% per year
- [Poupança](/glossario/poupanca): 6% per year
- Real gain: only 1%

## How to protect yourself?

- Invest in assets that yield above inflation
- Treasury IPCA+ guarantees real return
- Diversify between [renda fixa](/glossario/renda-fixa) and variable
- Track the monthly IPCA
